## Artistic Reading
- User image / premise: A 24-minute self-hypnosis journey guiding attention from external orientation to deep internal absorption and back, using structured hypnotic stages without vocal samples.
- Musical problem: Creating continuous field progression through harmonic, spatial, and textural evolution without abrupt changes or vocal mimicry.
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening, allowing attention to settle and absorb without monitoring the process.
- What this is not: Dramatic, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension, or sad narrative.
- Style-family fit: Sequenced Ambient (Carbon Based Lifeforms/Solar Fields tradition) with structured hypnotic architecture.

## Chosen Musical Premise
- Premise sentence: A continuous field of F2-centered harmonic warmth evolves through stage-specific textural and spatial shifts to guide the listener from external orientation into deep absorption and back, using a voice-adjacent carrier for entrainment.
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish → deepen → dissolve (with fractionation) → return.
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, deepening breath in a warm cave that opens to light and returns to stillness.
- Musical consequence: Harmonic field shifts from open F major (with C, Eb) to darker Db shadow color and back; spatial width expands then contracts; carrier phrases settle downward with breath-length timing.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows full hypnotic arc (3+4+6+7+2+1.5+0.5) with room for micro-developments and natural pacing of deep absorption.

## Hypnotic Function Architecture
- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and return.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation (3:00): baseline to light, settle and establish safety.
  - Induction (4:00): light to medium, pace and lead attention inward.
  - Deepening (6:00): medium to deep, reduce orienting load and widen space.
  - Core absorptive work (7:00): deep, sustain absorption with micro-developments.
  - Fractionation (2:00): deep to light-medium to deep, briefly lighten then return deeper.
  - Integration (1:30): medium to light, stabilize and prepare for reorientation.
  - Return (0:30): light to baseline, dissolve attention outward gently.
- Depth trajectory: Baseline (orientation) → light (induction) → medium (deepening start) → deep (core) → light-medium (fractionation lightening) → medium (integration) → baseline (return). Concrete parameters: harmonic shift from F home to Db contrast, density sparse→moderate→low→medium→sparse, brightness warm mid→darker→dark→brighter→warm mid.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Start with slow ground and sparse carrier (orientation), then lengthen rests, lower register, darken brightness, widen space to lead attention inward.
- Ratification plan: Carrier returns with downward settling contour at key depth thresholds (induction threshold, deepening stability point).
- Suggestion-compatible windows: 7:30-9:00, 13:00-18:30, 22:00-23:00.
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, alerting cues, or sudden brightness spikes.

## Material Inventory
### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Three-note falling-settling cell (C3-Ab2-G2) functioning as carrier memory.
- First form: Soft carrier phrase during orientation after ground established (0:45-1:30).
- Later forms: Lower-slower-deepening form (deepening stage), thinned-core-fragment (core absorptive work), clearer-integration-return (integration).

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F root with open C (F3-C4), Eb warmth (Eb3-Bb3), G add9 (G3-D4) — open voicings in low register.
- Contrast color: Db root with common-tone F (Db3-F4), Ab shadow (Ab3-Eb4) — darker, more suspended.
- Return color: F root with fewer color tones (only open C and G add9), softened upper partials, narrower spatial field.

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Warm F2 drone with slow beating (0.1 Hz) and long attack (8-45s), creating stable field.
- Event layer behavior: Rare submerged motif fragments (every 15-45s) and soft partial blooms.
- Noise / air behavior: Filtered high air at edges (slow opening/closing, 10-60s attack/release) creating space without brightness.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (orientation) → medium (induction) → wide (deepening/core) → medium-wide (fractionation) → narrow (integration/return).
- Motion behavior: Slow drift of air layer from rear to edges, no fast autopan (max 20-120s movement).
- Depth behavior: Near (orientation) → medium (induction) → deep (deepening/core) → near-medium (fractionation) → near (integration/return).

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath-like modulation (0.07-0.13 Hz) for ground/harmonic body, asymmetric shimmer (multiple incommensurable rates) for air.
- Change rule: Modulation depth decreases as piece deepens (core), increases slightly during fractionation lightening, reduces again for return.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1.
- Reference pitch-contour profile: reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1 (falling_settling dominant).
- Carrier instrument / layer: Warm low-mid reed-like MIDI carrier (bass clarinet patch, filtered 120-350 Hz).
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs (0.9s-3.3s) with soft attack and long release, downward settling.
- Contour behavior: Falling_settling primary (brief opening for fractionation).
- Weighting behavior: Emphasis via register warmth and longer decay, not gain.
- Return behavior: Returns at induction threshold (3:00), deepening stability point (7:00), core reassurance (13:00), fractionation re-deepening (21:00), integration (22:00).
- Salience limit: Below lead melody attention — carrier at -15 dB.

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny low-mid warmth bloom (0.1s, 235 Hz) paired with carrier release.
- First pairing: Orientation after first carrier phrase resolves (at 1:30).
- Withholding plan: Absent for at least 90 seconds after first pairing (until induction threshold).
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at induction threshold, deepening stability point, core reassurance, fractionation re-deepening, integration.
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn (235 Hz low-mid) but not salient enough to monitor.

## Development Grammar
- Establishment rule: Introduce ground, harmonic body, and first carrier phrase in orientation.
- Variation rule: Material changes via harmonic shift (F to Db), density change, spatial widening.
- Contrast rule: Use contrast color (Db) for deepening/core, return to home.
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transformed (lowered, thinned) at key stages.
- Foreground rule: Event layer becomes legible once per 60-120 seconds (micro-event).
- Withholding rule: Anchor absent for 90s after first pairing; core absorptive work withholds carrier.
- Fractionation rule: Brief lightening via increased brightness/density, immediate re-deepening through darker carrier return.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with comfort (carrier release) to condition as deepening marker.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final dissolve through harmonic simplification (F root only) and spatial contraction.
- Anti-padding rule: No looped events; all changes intentional micro-developments.

## Orchestration Map
- Root / ground function: F2 drone (ground_layer).
- Harmonic color function: Dark low-mid pad (harmonic_body_layer) with F-centered voicings.
- Motion function: Carrier layer for weighted cadence and depth progression.
- Foreground / event function: Event_layer (rare motif fragments).
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Carrier layer.
- Anchor / trigger function: Anchor_layer (low-mid warmth bloom).
- Air / bloom function: Air_bloom_layer.
- Spatial / depth function: Layer placement (ground centered, harmonic_body wide, carrier near-center, air at edges).
- Silence / restraint function: Withholding of anchor and carrier in core.

## Temporal Behavior
- Micro-event rate: Every 15-45 seconds (rare motif fragments, air blooms).
- Scene-change rate: Every 75-150 seconds (harmonic shifts, spatial changes).
- Long-wave change: Whole piece moves from narrow to wide to narrow.
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without deliberate change (to avoid inertness).
- Repetition policy: Recurrence of motif transformed, not identical.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: First at 1:30, then returns at 3:00 (induction), 7:00 (deepening), 13:00 (core), 21:00 (fractionation re-deepening), 22:00 (integration).

## Spatial Composition
- Stereo requirement: Stereo.
- Spatial arc: Narrow → medium → wide → medium-wide → narrow (with slow air layer drift).
- Layer placement principles: ground centered, harmonic_body wide_stable, carrier near_center_low_motion, air edges_and_rear, anchor local_but_soft.
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; motion over 20-120 seconds only for tiny anchor gestures.
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, not dual-mono; width arc audible and motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria
- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: no perceptible change for >45 seconds.
  - Revision target: add micro-event or density shift.

- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: could be any ambient piece without specific harmonic signature.
  - Revision target: strengthen home/contrast colors and motif.

- Failure mode: too busy
  - Evidence: more than one event per 15 seconds.
  - Revision target: reduce event layer density.

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: motif appears once and vanishes.
  - Revision target: transform motif at each return.

- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: fades out without harmonic simplification.
  - Revision target: add F root only and spatial contraction.

- Failure mode: weak spatial composition
  - Evidence: mono or static width.
  - Revision target: add stereo construction and verify arc.

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: no clear stage progression.
  - Revision target: align harmonic, spatial, and carrier changes to stage map.

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: lightening feels like reset.
  - Revision target: ensure continuity bed under pocket.

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: anchor too obvious or random.
  - Revision target: soften onset and add withholding.

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: carrier behaves like lead melody.
  - Revision target: lower salience and add permissive pauses.
