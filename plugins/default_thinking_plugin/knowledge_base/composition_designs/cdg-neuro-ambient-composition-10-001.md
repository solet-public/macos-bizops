## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A 24-minute music-only self-hypnosis journey guiding attention from external orientation to protected internal absorption and back, with no vocal content.
- Musical problem: How to create non-verbal depth progression without rhythmic pulse or abrupt transitions that disrupt absorption.
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening, not tracking the music but being absorbed by it.
- What this is not: Dramatic, sad narrative, triumphant, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension.
- Style-family fit: Sequenced Ambient (Carbon Based Lifeforms/Solar Fields) fits through long-form harmonic evolution and texture-based development without hooks.

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A 24-minute harmonic and spatial journey using a voice-adjacent carrier to guide depth progression through weighted breath-length phrases, with the ocean current metaphor translating to widening harmonic space and subtle eddies as fractionation.
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish -> deepen -> fractionate (brief lightening) -> return
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, deep ocean current carrying the listener inward, with gentle eddies (fractionation) and a warm glow at depth.
- Musical consequence: Ocean current = harmonic expansion (wider intervals) and spatial widening; eddies = brief lightening events with controlled return.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows full hypnotic arc (orientation, induction, deepening, core work) with time to build and sustain absorption without rushing.

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for absorption and reorientation.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation (3:00): baseline to light, settle and establish safety
  - Induction (4:00): light to medium, pace and lead attention inward
  - Deepening (6:00): medium to deep, reduce orienting load and widen space
  - Core absorptive work (7:00): deep, sustain absorption with micro-developments
  - Fractionation (2:00): deep to light-medium to deep, briefly lighten then return deeper
  - Integration (1:30): medium to light, stabilize and prepare for reorientation
  - Return (0:30): light to baseline, dissolve attention outward gently
- Depth trajectory: Baseline (orientation) → Light (induction start) → Medium (deepening start) → Deep (core work) → Light-medium (fractionation lightening) → Deep (fractionation return) → Medium (integration start) → Light (return start) → Baseline (end)
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Start with sparse carrier phrases and narrow field; lengthen rests, lower register, darken brightness, widen space to lead attention inward.
- Ratification plan: Recurring carrier phrases with downward settling at depth thresholds (induction, deepening stability, core reassurance) to confirm state.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: 7:30-9:00, 13:00-18:30, 22:00-23:00 (where spoken guidance could be added without masking).
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, alerting cues, or sudden brightness spikes.

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Two-note interval cell (minor third followed by falling second), minor third in low register, falling second ascending.
- First form: Sparse event (once every 45 seconds) in harmonic body layer during orientation.
- Later forms: Transposed up an octave for induction; widened and resonant in deepening; thinned and embedded in air layer during core work.

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F root with open C (F-C) and Eb warmth (F-Eb), forming dark warm F minor 7th chord without fifth.
- Contrast color: Db root with common-tone F (Db-F), forming luminous shadow Db major 7th chord.
- Return color: F root with only C and Eb (no G/Bb), softened upper partials, narrower spatial field.

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Slow-breathing harmonic body pad with irregular micro-swells (every 15-45 seconds).
- Event layer behavior: Rare submerged motif fragments and soft partial blooms appearing only at harmonic body peaks.
- Noise / air behavior: Filtered high air (pink noise above 2800 Hz) opening slowly at edges with long release.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (orientation) → Medium (induction) → Wide (deepening) → Medium-wide (core work) → Narrow (integration and return).
- Motion behavior: Slow drifting in air layer; anchor cues move locally but softly.
- Depth behavior: Near (orientation) → Medium (induction) → Deep (deepening/core work) → Near (integration) → Dissolved (return).

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath-like modulation (0.07-0.13 Hz) for harmonic body, nested asymmetric shimmer.
- Change rule: Modulation rate increases during deepening, recedes during core work; shimmer becomes more irregular as depth increases.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1.
- Reference pitch-contour profile: falling_settling for induction/deepening, scoop_then_settle for permissive gestures.
- Carrier instrument / layer: Warm low-mid reed-like MIDI pad (GM program 89, F4 aeolian walk).
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs (0.9s to 3.3s) with weighted downward settling.
- Contour behavior: Predominantly falling_settling, especially during deepening and return; occasional scoop_then_settle.
- Weighting behavior: Emphasis on longer decay and slight register warmth, not gain.
- Return behavior: Returns transformed (slower, darker) at induction threshold, deepening stability point, core reassurance, fractionation re-deepening, integration.
- Salience limit: Below lead melody attention (sub-salient).

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny low-mid warmth bloom (0.5s) paired with carrier release, using soft F-Eb interval.
- First pairing: Orientation after first carrier phrase resolves (at 0:45).
- Withholding plan: Absent for at least 90 seconds after first pairing (until induction threshold).
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at induction threshold (3:00), deepening stability point (7:00), core reassurance (13:00), fractionation re-deepening (21:00), integration (22:00).
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn (15 dB below carrier), not salient enough to cause monitoring.

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce ground drone, harmonic body (home color), and first carrier phrase in orientation.
- Variation rule: Transform motif by register (low to high) and density (sparse to dense) across stages.
- Contrast rule: Shift harmonic color from home (F minor 7th) to contrast (Db major 7th) during deepening, then return.
- Recurrence rule: Motif returns transformed every 15-45 seconds in core work; anchor returns at depth thresholds.
- Foreground rule: Event layer becomes slightly more legible during fractionation lightening phase only.
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld until induction threshold; fractionation brightness withheld during core work.
- Fractionation rule: Brief lightening (20:00-21:00) with increased brightness/density, then re-deepening (21:00-22:00) with darker filter/wider space.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier release during orientation; return at depth thresholds to reinforce state.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final dissolution requires harmonic simplification (return color), spatial contraction, and event-rate reduction.
- Anti-padding rule: No material looped merely to fill time; every micro-event serves development.

### Formal Unit 5 - Fractionation Pocket (20:00-22:00)

- Spatial posture: Medium (lightening) → Wide (re-deepening).

### Formal Unit 6 - Integration (22:00-23:30)
- Name / role: Return familiar material in clearer calm form.

- Hypnotic function: Stabilize and prepare for reorientation.

- Spatial posture: Medium, carrier near center.
- Transition condition: Fractionation re-deepened.

- Name / role: Dissolve and cadence.
- Approximate duration range: 25s-35s

- Hypnotic function: Reorient attention outward gently.
- Energy / density posture: Very low, dissolving energy.
- Harmonic / spectral posture: Return color with no upper partials.
- Spatial posture: Narrow, air layer fading to rear.
- Transition condition: Integration cadence prepared.

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: F2 drone (87.31 Hz) with slow beating.
- Harmonic color function: Dark low-mid pad (F-centered open voicings).
- Motion function: Slow filter motion and density change.
- Foreground / event function: Rare motif fragments (two-note cell).
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Warm low-mid reed-like MIDI carrier (GM 89).
- Anchor / trigger function: Low-mid warmth bloom paired with carrier release.
- Air / bloom function: Filtered high air (pink noise above 2800 Hz).
- Spatial / depth function: Stereo width and layer placement (narrow to wide to narrow arc).
- Silence / restraint function: Withholding carrier during core work.

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: Every 15-45 seconds (motif fragments, partial blooms).
- Scene-change rate: Every 75-150 seconds (harmonic region shifts, spatial changes).
- Long-wave change: Harmonic color shift from home to contrast during deepening.
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without deliberate change or withholding.
- Repetition policy: Motif returns transformed; no identical repetition.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: First pairing (0:45), then returns at 3:00, 7:00, 13:00, 21:00, 22:00.

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: Stereo.
- Spatial arc: Narrow (orientation) → Medium (induction) → Wide (deepening) → Medium-wide (core work) → Narrow (integration and return).
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered; harmonic body wide stable; carrier near center low motion; air at edges and rear.
- Motion limits: No fast autopan (motion over 20-120 seconds only for tiny anchor gestures).
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, dual-mono; width arc audible and motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No perceptible change for >45 seconds in core work.
  - Revision target: Add micro-event (motif fragment) or density shift.

- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: Motif is a vague "ambient texture" without identity.
  - Revision target: Define primary motif as two-note interval cell.

- Failure mode: too busy
  - Evidence: Carrier phrases every 10 seconds, masking absorption.
  - Revision target: Increase carrier rest duration to [0.5-1.8] seconds.

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Motif appears once and vanishes.
  - Revision target: Return motif transformed in core work.

- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: Piece fades out without harmonic simplification.
  - Revision target: Add return color and spatial contraction.

- Failure mode: weak spatial composition
  - Evidence: Output is mono or width doesn't change.
  - Revision target: Implement stereo construction with layer placement.

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: No clear depth progression (e.g., deepening stage doesn't deepen).
  - Revision target: Adjust harmonic color and spatial width for each stage.

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like a reset (e.g., new section).
  - Revision target: Keep continuity bed under fractionation; bind lightening to brightness only.

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor is too obvious (e.g., bright click).
  - Revision target: Soften anchor to low-mid warmth bloom at 15 dB below carrier.

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier is a lead melody (e.g., rhythmic, bright).
  - Revision target: Use warm reed-like pad with breath-length phrases.
