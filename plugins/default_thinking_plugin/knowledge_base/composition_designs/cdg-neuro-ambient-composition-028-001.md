## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A 24-minute music-only self-hypnosis journey guiding attention from external orientation into protected internal absorption and back to stable calm.
- Musical problem: How to create a continuous field that guides depth without words, using musical elements as hypnotic anchors.
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening (broad environmental → internal absorption → gentle reorientation).
- What this is not: Dramatic, sad narrative, triumphant, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension.
- Style-family fit: Sequenced ambient (Carbon Based Lifeforms/Solar Fields) with long-form development.

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A slow descent into a resonant field where harmonic motion and spatial expansion guide attention inward, with voice-adjacent carrier as a weighted cadence for depth ratification.
- Primary dramatic motion: Deepen (with fractionation as a return to deeper).
- Sound-world metaphor: A warm cave that gently opens to light and contracts back inward, never breaking the field.
- Musical consequence: Harmonic motion via moving bass roots (D2 → F#2 → A2), spectral darkening (filter 3200→1800 Hz), spatial widening (narrow → wide), and texture thinning.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows depth trajectory (baseline → light → medium → deep → brief lightening → deeper return) to unfold with sufficient time for each stage and fractionation.

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and gentle return.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation: 0:00-3:00 (baseline to light) — settle and establish safety
  - Induction: 3:00-7:00 (light to medium) — pace and lead attention
  - Deepening: 7:00-13:00 (medium to deep) — reduce orienting load
  - Core absorptive work: 13:00-20:00 (deep) — sustain absorption
  - Fractionation pocket: 20:00-22:00 (deep to light-medium to deep) — briefly lighten then return deeper
  - Integration: 22:00-23:30 (medium to light) — stabilize
  - Return: 23:30-24:00 (light to baseline) — reorient
- Depth trajectory: Concrete parameters: Baseline = bright (3200 Hz), narrow width, sparse events; Light = medium brightness, medium width; Medium = dark (2800 Hz), wider; Deep = darkest (1800 Hz), wide, low density; Light-medium = brighter (2500 Hz), narrower; Deep = returns to 1800 Hz, wide; Baseline = bright (3200 Hz), narrow.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: First pace broad listening with slow ground and sparse carrier, then lengthen rests (0.5s→1.8s), lower register, darken brightness, widen space.
- Ratification plan: Voice-adjacent carrier returns at depth thresholds (induction, deepening stability) with downward settling contour to ratify state.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: 7:30-9:00, 13:00-18:30, 22:00-23:00.
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, alerting cues, or sudden brightness spikes.

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Three-note falling interval cell (minor third + minor second) in low-mid register.
- First form: Sparse occurrence at 0:45 (single phrase arc, long rests).
- Later forms: Transposed up minor third in deepening; reduced to two notes in core work; brighter and wider in fractionation pocket.

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: D2 (73.42 Hz) + F#2 + A2 (D major triad, bass root moves).
- Contrast color: G2 (98 Hz) + B2 + D3 (G major triad, fourth above home).
- Return color: Home color with fifth removed and seventh added (D minor 7th).

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Warm F2 drone (8 Hz beat, long attack/release).
- Event layer behavior: Rare motif fragments and partial blooms at micro-event intervals.
- Noise / air behavior: Filtered high air (10 kHz) opening/closing with slow release.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (0:00) → medium (3:00) → wide (7:00) → medium-wide (13:00) → narrow (24:00).
- Motion behavior: Slow drifting in air layer; no fast autopan.
- Depth behavior: Near (0:00) → medium (7:00) → far (13:00) → near (24:00).

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath (0.07 Hz) for ground layer; asymmetric shimmer for air.
- Change rule: Modulation rate slows, depth recedes over time.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1.
- Reference pitch-contour profile: reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1.
- Carrier instrument / layer: Sarangi-like bowed string (low-mid, warm timbre).
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs (0.9s–3.3s), soft attack/release.
- Contour behavior: Falling-settling (downward interval + longer decay).
- Weighting behavior: Slight emphasis on downward note, permissive pauses.
- Return behavior: Returns at induction threshold (3:00), deepening stability (10:00), core reassurance (16:00), fractionation re-deepening (21:00), integration (23:00).
- Salience limit: -15 dB, sub-salient (never lead element).

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Low-mid warmth bloom (0.5s swell at 200 Hz).
- First pairing: At orientation end (2:45), carrier settles downward and anchor blooms at release.
- Withholding plan: Absent 90s after first pairing (until induction threshold).
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at depth thresholds (3:00, 10:00, 16:00, 21:00, 23:00).
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn (at -18 dB), not salient enough to monitor.

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce ground drone, harmonic body pad, and carrier in orientation.
- Variation rule: Gradually darken brightness (filter 3200→1800 Hz) and widen space over induction/deepening.
- Contrast rule: Shift to contrast color (G major) during fractionation pocket, return to home.
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transformed at each major stage (induction, deepening, core).
- Foreground rule: Only motif and anchor become perceptible; all else sub-salient.
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld 90s after first pairing; carrier absent during core work except at thresholds.
- Fractionation rule: Lightening (20:00–21:00) with brighter filter and wider width; re-deepening (21:00–22:00) with darker filter and narrower width.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier settling; returns only at stable depth states.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final fade-out preceded by harmonic simplification (home color) and spatial contraction.
- Anti-padding rule: No loops/stretches; all changes developmentally motivated.

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: F2 drone (warm, slow beating).
- Harmonic color function: Harmonic body pad (F-centered open voicings, slow filter motion).
- Motion function: Bass root sequence generator (D2 → F#2 → A2) and arpeggio pattern.
- Foreground / event function: Primary motif (three-note falling cell) and anchor bloom.
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Sarangi-like carrier (breath-length phrases, downward settling).
- Anchor / trigger function: Low-mid warmth bloom (paired with carrier settling).
- Air / bloom function: Filtered high air layer (slow opening/closing).
- Spatial / depth function: Stereo width and depth controlled by layer placement.
- Silence / restraint function: Withholding carrier during core work, limiting event density.

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: Every 15-45 seconds (motif fragments, air blooms).
- Scene-change rate: Every 75-150 seconds (harmonic shifts, density changes).
- Long-wave change: Spectral arc from 3200 Hz → 1800 Hz → 3200 Hz.
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without deliberate change.
- Repetition policy: Only motif and anchor recur, transformed each time.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: First pairing (2:45), returns at 3:00, 10:00, 16:00, 21:00, 23:00.

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: stereo.
- Spatial arc: Narrow → medium → wide → medium-wide → narrow (with slow air layer drift).
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered, harmonic body wide stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges/rear, anchor local soft.
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; motion over 20-120 seconds allowed for air layer.
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, not dual-mono; width arc audible and motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: no perceptible change for >45 seconds.
  - Revision target: add micro-event or density shift.

- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: could be any ambient piece.
  - Revision target: add specific motif and harmonic signature.

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: lightening feels like reset.
  - Revision target: ensure continuity bed under fractionation.

- Failure mode: weak anchors
  - Evidence: anchor too obvious or random.
  - Revision target: soften onset and move to depth thresholds.

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: carrier too bright or mechanical.
  - Revision target: lower salience to -15 dB and use falling-settling contour.
