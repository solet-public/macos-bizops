## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A continuous, breath-led instrumental journey through hypnotic stages for self-hypnosis.
- Musical problem: Creating continuous form that guides listeners through seven hypnotic stages without abrupt changes while maintaining a warm spectral posture and perceptible prosodic lead.
- Listener stance: Following the continuous breath-length cadence without active monitoring or attention tracking.
- What this is not: Dramatic, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension, or a piece with percussion/groove.
- Style-family fit: Neuro-ambient self-hypnosis adapted from the "warm, slightly dark spectral posture" style family with voice-adjacent induction.

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A continuous breath-led instrumental journey through seven hypnotic stages using a descending interval cell motif and harmonic region shifts to guide the listener from orientation through deep absorption to gentle return.
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish → deepen → dissolve (with fractionation as brief lightening before deeper return).
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, deep ocean current carrying the listener through layers of water without breaking surface tension.
- Musical consequence: The descending interval cell motif becomes the anchor for depth; harmonic regions shift to create stage transitions (narrowing → widening); spatial width evolves from intimate to expansive then back.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes earned through seven-stage architecture (each stage requiring time to develop depth) and micro-variation preventing stasis without breaking trance.

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and return.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 0:00-3:00, target depth: baseline-to-light, musical function: establish tonal anchor and first carrier pairing.
  - Induction / Narrowing: 3:00-7:00, target depth: light-to-medium, musical function: pace attention inward through rhythmic cadence.
  - Deepening: 7:00-13:00, target depth: medium-to-deep, musical function: widen space and lower carrier register to reduce orienting load.
  - Core Absorptive Work: 13:00-20:00, target depth: deep, musical function: sustain absorption with subtle micro-developments.
  - Fractionation Pocket: 20:00-22:00, target depth: deep-to-light-medium-to-deep, musical function: briefly lighten attention then return deeper without reset.
  - Integration / Stabilization: 22:00-23:30, target depth: medium-to-light, musical function: stabilize with familiar carrier and harmonic return.
  - Return / Reorientation: 23:30-24:00, target depth: light-to-baseline, musical function: dissolve field with spectral narrowing.
- Depth trajectory: Baseline (orientation) → light (induction) → medium (deepening start) → deep (core work) → brief light-medium (fractionation) → medium-light (integration) → baseline (return). Achieved through harmonic region shifts, spatial widening/narrowing, and carrier register descent.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Carrier establishes breath-length cadence (6–14s phrases) in orientation; gradually shortens phrase intervals to narrow attention during induction.
- Ratification plan: Descending interval cell motif returns transformed (transposed down minor third) at depth thresholds to ratify accepted state.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: Quietest moments (10s before motif return) left for spoken guidance without masking.
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt changes in harmonic color, spatial width, or carrier register; all transitions occur over 15-30 seconds.

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Two-note interval cell (minor third) descending then rising slightly to settle, appearing as soft glint in harmonic bed.
- First form: At 0:45, F3 and A#3 played as soft glint (2s decay).
- Later forms: Transposed down minor third at 7:00 (deepening), transposed up major second at 22:15 (integration).

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: Root F2 (87.31 Hz) with interval stack [0, 3, 10] (root, minor third, major tenth).
- Contrast color: Root F2 with interval stack [0, 5, 9] (root, major third, ninth) during induction/fractionation.
- Return color: Home color with tenth softened to major ninth ([0, 3, 9]) during integration/return.

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Low-mid substrate (F2) with progressive gain reduction over 10 minutes.
- Event layer behavior: Primary motif glints (every 15–45 seconds) and rare carrier fragments during core work.
- Noise / air behavior: Soft, wide air layer opening only after motif glint and closing over 10 seconds.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (25%) → medium (40%) → wide (70%) → medium-wide (55%) → narrow-medium (35%) → intimate (20%) → narrow (15%).
- Motion behavior: Slow air layer drift (0.5°/second), no fast autopan.
- Depth behavior: Harmonic bed fixed at 10m depth; carrier moves from 5m to 2m during deepening.

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Long breath (0.07–0.13 Hz) for substrate; gentle body pulse (0.15–0.35 Hz) for harmonic bed.
- Change rule: Breath rate slows by 0.01 Hz every 5 minutes; body pulse depth increases by 2% over piece.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: `reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1` (6–14s phrases, 84.5% falling-settling).
- Reference pitch-contour profile: `reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1` (84.5% falling-settling).
- Carrier instrument / layer: Soft cello with warm low-mid timbre (GM program 89), filtered for chest resonance.
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrases with 1–3s inter-phrase breaths, weighted stress on second note.
- Contour behavior: 84.5% falling-settling (small rising opening at fractionation), no arch/level contours.
- Weighting behavior: Slight sustain increase on second note for emphasis without loudness.
- Return behavior: Carrier returns with transformed contour (slower, darker) at depth thresholds.
- Salience limit: 6dB above harmonic bed and 10dB above substrate, never becoming foreground melody.

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny spectral unsealing (0.5s brightening at 12kHz) paired with carrier phrase ending.
- First pairing: At 0:45 (orientation), anchor appears after first carrier phrase.
- Withholding plan: Anchor absent during induction (3:00–7:00) to make return meaningful.
- Return / trigger plan: Anchor returns at 7:15 (deepening), 20:30 (fractionation return), 22:45 (integration).
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn (0.5dB above bed), not salient enough to cause monitoring.

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Establish home color and carrier in orientation with one motif glint.
- Variation rule: Harmonic bed shifts to contrast color during induction; carrier register lowers in deepening.
- Contrast rule: Harmonic bed shifts from home to contrast color (induction) and back to return color (integration).
- Recurrence rule: Motif glint returns transposed down minor third at deepening (7:00) and up major second at integration (22:15).
- Foreground rule: Carrier always foreground but never becomes melody (stays within 6dB above bed).
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld during induction to make return meaningful.
- Fractionation rule: During fractionation (20:00–22:00), carrier briefly brightens and opens spatially, then returns deeper via darker contour.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier phrase endings during orientation to create conditioned association.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Spectral narrowing (12kHz → 8kHz) and spatial contraction over last 30 seconds.
- Anti-padding rule: No event repeated within 15 seconds; all events spaced to prevent wallpaper.

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: Substrate (F2 anchor) grounds the listener.
- Harmonic color function: Harmonic bed supplies pitch identity and stage transitions.
- Motion function: Carrier (cello) drives attention inward/outward through prosodic lead.
- Foreground / event function: Motif glints are occasional foreground events, spaced to avoid wallpaper.
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Carrier (cello) carries weighted cadence and downward settling.
- Anchor / trigger function: Spectral unsealing (anchor) paired with carrier phrase endings creates conditioned markers.
- Air / bloom function: Air layer creates openness, opening only after motif glints.
- Spatial / depth function: Harmonic bed width and carrier depth create spatial arc.
- Silence / restraint function: Anchor withheld during induction to make return meaningful.

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One motif glint every 15–45 seconds (e.g., 0:45, 3:20, 6:15).
- Scene-change rate: Harmonic region shifts every 4–7 minutes (induction at 3:00, deepening at 7:00).
- Long-wave change: Breath rate slows by 0.01 Hz every 5 minutes; body pulse depth increases.
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without micro-event or harmonic shift (e.g., core work).
- Repetition policy: Motif glints return transformed, not identical; no event repeats within 15 seconds.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening at 20:00 (brief brightening/spatial opening), return deeper at 21:30.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: At 0:45 (orientation), 7:15 (deepening), 20:30 (fractionation return), 22:45 (integration).

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: Stereo.
- Spatial arc: Narrow → medium → wide → medium-wide → narrow-medium → intimate → narrow (as defined in Formal Architecture).
- Layer placement principles: Substrate centered, harmonic bed wide-stable, carrier near-center-low-motion, air at edges/rear.
- Motion limits: Air layer drifts 0.5°/second; no fast autopan.
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible and motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No micro-events for >45 seconds in core work.
  - Revision target: Add motif glint at 16:20 and 18:55.
- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: Harmonic bed uses same interval stack throughout.
  - Revision target: Shift to contrast color during induction and return color in integration.
- Failure mode: too busy
  - Evidence: Motif glints every 10 seconds.
  - Revision target: Space motif glints to 25–45 seconds apart.
- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Motif glint returns identical at deepening.
  - Revision target: Transpose motif down minor third at 7:00.
- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: Piece fades without spectral narrowing.
  - Revision target: Add spectral narrowing (12kHz → 8kHz) over last 30 seconds.
- Failure mode: weak spatial composition
  - Evidence: Width remains at 50% throughout.
  - Revision target: Implement full spatial arc (narrow to wide to narrow).
- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: Fractionation pocket feels like new section.
  - Revision target: Bind fractionation lightening to carrier contour (brief brightening) and return deeper via wider spatial arc.
- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Fractionation lightening abrupt and reset-like.
  - Revision target: Lighten over 15 seconds, return deeper with wider spatial arc.
- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor too obvious (1dB above bed).
  - Revision target: Reduce anchor to 0.5dB above bed and shorten to 1s.
- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier phrases metrical (4s each).
  - Revision target: Use breath-length phrases (6–14s) with permissive pauses.
