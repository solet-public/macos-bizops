## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: Slow descent into deep resonant inner space through layered breath-like textures and harmonic motion
- Musical problem: Creating continuous hypnotic progression without attention-grabbing events or abrupt transitions in a 24-minute self-hypnosis piece
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening with attention shifting from environmental to internal absorption
- What this is not: Dramatic, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension, or sad narrative
- Style-family fit: Sequenced Ambient (Carbon Based Lifeforms/Solar Fields) with hypnotic architecture for self-hypnosis

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A 24-minute journey through harmonic region shifts and breath-like textures that guides the listener from baseline awareness to deep absorption and gentle return through structured hypnotic architecture
- Primary dramatic motion: Deepen (with transformation) then dissolve into return
- Sound-world metaphor: Descending through resonant cavern layers where harmonic shifts feel like geological strata
- Musical consequence: Harmonic region darkening (lowpass filter cutoff reduction), spatial widening then contraction, and motif transposition downward as depth increases
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes earned through multi-stage hypnotic architecture (7 stages) with harmonic evolution requiring slow development to support deep absorption

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and return
- Stage map:
  - Orientation (3:00): baseline-to-light, settle and establish safety
  - Induction (4:00): light-to-medium, pace and lead attention inward
  - Deepening (6:00): medium-to-deep, reduce orienting load and widen space
  - Core absorptive work (7:00): deep, sustain absorption with micro-developments
  - Fractionation (2:00): deep-to-light-medium-to-deep, briefly lighten then return deeper
  - Integration (1:30): medium-to-light, stabilize and prepare for reorientation
  - Return (0:30): light-to-baseline, gently reorient attention outward
- Depth trajectory: Harmonic brightness decreases (cutoff 12kHz → 8kHz), spatial width increases then contracts, event density rises slightly in core work
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Start with slow ground (87.31 Hz) and sparse carrier phrases; lengthen rests, lower register, darken brightness (filter), widen space
- Ratification plan: Carrier returns with downward settling contour at end of each stage to ratify depth achieved
- Suggestion-compatible windows: Quietest moments after carrier phrases (0:45, 3:15, 7:20, 13:10, 20:45)
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, alerting cues, or sudden brightness spikes

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Two-note minor third interval (F3-A#3) followed by falling second (A#3-G3), asymmetric gap
- First form: Air layer glint at 0:45 (1.2s attack, 6s release)
- Later forms: Transposed down by minor third at 7:00, filtered and sparse in core work

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F2 (87.31 Hz) + C3 (174.62 Hz) + A#3 (207.65 Hz)
- Contrast color: Bb2 (146.83 Hz) + C#3 (155.56 Hz) + F3 (174.62 Hz)
- Return color: Original home with minor seventh removed, fifth softened (3dB lower)

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Ground drone (87.31 Hz) with 20s attack, 60s release
- Event layer behavior: Primary motif glints every 20-45 seconds in air layer
- Noise / air behavior: Pink noise high-passed at 2800 Hz, slow attack (60s), release (90s)

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow → medium-wide → wide (fractionation) → medium → narrow
- Motion behavior: Air layer glints move left to right over 120 seconds during deepening
- Depth behavior: Ground centered, harmonic body widens to medium depth in deepening

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath (0.07-0.13 Hz) for ground, asymmetric shimmer (0.15Hz + 0.25Hz)
- Change rule: Breath rate slows by 0.01 Hz every 3 minutes, shimmer becomes less regular

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1
- Reference pitch-contour profile: falling_settling dominant
- Carrier instrument / layer: Sarangi-like bowed string patch (low-mid register)
- Cadence behavior: 1.5-2.3s phrases with 0.75s permissive pauses
- Contour behavior: Predominantly falling_settling, occasional scoop_then_settle
- Weighting behavior: Emphasis via register warmth and harmonic reinforcement (not gain)
- Return behavior: Returns with more submerged contour (lower register, longer decay)
- Salience limit: Carrier volume ceiling -15 dB

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: 0.5s spectral unsealing in air layer (upper partial brightening)
- First pairing: At 0:45 with carrier's downward settling phrase
- Withholding plan: Anchor withheld for 10 minutes after first pairing (until deepening)
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at 13:00 (core work) and 20:00 (fractionation return)
- Salience policy: Audible at -25 dB, not salient enough to cause monitoring

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce ground drone and carrier in orientation with sparse motif
- Variation rule: Primary motif returns transposed down by minor third during deepening
- Contrast rule: Contrast color appears only in deepening as harmonic shift
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif recurs every 20-45 seconds with increasing transformation
- Foreground rule: Carrier is only foreground element (at low salience)
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld for 10 minutes after first pairing
- Fractionation rule: Air layer brightens slightly (30s) then returns to darker state
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier's downward settling at 0:45
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Ends with carrier's downward settling phrase at 23:45
- Anti-padding rule: No event repeated merely to fill time; all events part of development

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: Ground drone (87.31 Hz) at center
- Harmonic color function: Harmonic body with contrast/home colors
- Motion function: Air layer glints (spatial movement) and modulation rates
- Foreground / event function: Carrier layer (voice-adjacent)
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Sarangi-like carrier
- Anchor / trigger function: Air layer glint (anchor cue)
- Air / bloom function: Noise air layer
- Spatial / depth function: Layer placement (ground centered, harmonic body wide)
- Silence / restraint function: Withholding of anchor and carrier in core work

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: Every 20-45 seconds (primary motif)
- Scene-change rate: Every 6 minutes (stage transitions)
- Long-wave change: Harmonic region shifts and spatial arc
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without change (core work)
- Repetition policy: Primary motif recurs with transformation; no identical loops
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00
- Anchor recurrence spacing: First at 0:45, then 13:00 and 20:00

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: Stereo
- Spatial arc: Narrow → medium-wide → wide (fractionation) → medium → narrow
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered, harmonic body wide stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges and rear
- Motion limits: Air layer glints move slowly (120s) to avoid alerting
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible and motion not alerting

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No perceptible change for >45 seconds in core work
  - Revision target: Add transformed recurrence of primary motif or adjust modulation rates

- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: Could be any ambient piece; no specific motif or harmonic signature
  - Revision target: Strengthen primary motif identity and harmonic contrast

- Failure mode: too busy
  - Evidence: Carrier or air layer events exceed 1 per 20 seconds
  - Revision target: Reduce event density to one every 30-45 seconds

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Primary motif appears once and vanishes
  - Revision target: Add two transformed returns (at 7:00 and 13:00)

- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: Piece fades out without cadence
  - Revision target: Compose final carrier phrase (downward settling) at 23:45

- Failure mode: weak spatial composition
  - Evidence: Output mono or width doesn't change
  - Revision target: Add stereo construction for air layer movement

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: Stages feel like separate sections without depth trajectory
  - Revision target: Tighten harmonic and spatial controls per stage

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Fractionation lightening feels like reset
  - Revision target: Ensure continuity bed (ground drone) unchanged during fractionation

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor cue too obvious or random
  - Revision target: Soften anchor (volume -25 dB) and pair with carrier phrase

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier behaves like lead melody
  - Revision target: Reduce carrier salience to -15 dB and use falling_settling contours
