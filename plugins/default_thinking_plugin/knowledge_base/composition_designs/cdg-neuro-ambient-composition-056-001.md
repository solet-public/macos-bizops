## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A continuous breath-length instrumental lead voice guides the listener through a self-hypnotic journey from orientation to return, evoking calm internal absorption
- Musical problem: Creating a self-hypnotic journey without abrupt changes while maintaining deepening continuity through instrumental means
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening with attention directed internally rather than externally
- What this is not: Dramatic, sad narrative, triumphant, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension
- Style-family fit: Neuro-ambient with voice-adjacent induction and subsalient nonverbal anchors

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A continuous breath-length carrier voice guides the listener through seven hypnotic stages via harmonic evolution, spatial widening, and subtle anchor conditioning without any percussive or transient elements
- Primary dramatic motion: Deepen (through harmonic and spatial evolution rather than escalation)
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, warm river flowing through a forest at dusk
- Musical consequence: The metaphor changes harmony via stable F2 anchor with slow harmonic shifts (home color: F major 7th), texture through continuous substrate with irregular micro-blooms, register by staying low-mid and gradually darkening, space through widening then contracting
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes is musically justified by the seven-stage structure requiring time for deepening (6:00), core absorption (7:00), and fractionation's lightening/deeper-return cycle

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / settling: 0:00-3:00, baseline-to-light depth, establish world and first carrier pairing
  - Induction / narrowing: 3:00-7:00, light-to-medium depth, pace and lead attention inward
  - Deepening: 7:00-13:00, medium-to-deep depth, widen space and stabilize root memory
  - Core absorptive work: 13:00-20:00, deep depth, sustain absorption with non-inert microdevelopment
  - Fractionation pocket: 20:00-22:00, deep-to-light-medium-to-deep depth, briefly lighten then return deeper
  - Integration: 22:00-23:30, medium-to-light depth, return familiar material in calmer form
  - Return / reorientation: 23:30-24:00, light-to-baseline depth, dissolve and cadence
- Depth trajectory: Baseline (orientation) → light (induction) → medium (deepening) → deep (core) → light-medium (fractionation) → medium-light (integration) → baseline (return)
- Pacing-and-leading plan: First pace broad listening with slow ground and sparse carrier, then lengthen rests, lower register, darken brightness, widen space
- Ratification plan: Carrier voice returns with downward settling contour at each stage threshold (induction, deepening stability, core reassurance)
- Suggestion-compatible windows: Quiet moments between carrier phrases (after settling contour) where spoken guidance could sit without masking
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, or alerting brightness spikes

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Falling minor third (F to D) followed by rising second (D to E)
- First form: Sparse low-mid carrier phrase (0.9s duration) with soft attack in orientation
- Later forms: Transposed down by minor third in deepening, widened spatially in core, filtered to warmer resonance in integration

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F2 (87.31 Hz) with open voicing of F major 7th (F-A-C-E)
- Contrast color: Bb minor 9th (Bb-D-F-G) introduced in induction for gentle harmonic shift
- Return color: F major 7th with E lowered to D (F-A-C-D) for warmer resolution in integration

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer: Low-mid substrate with slow irregular micro-blooms (every 20-45s)
- Event layer: Submerged primary motif fragments appearing only in core and fractionation stages
- Noise / air: High-frequency air layer opening slowly (over 10-20s) in deepening, closing in integration

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (10%) → wide (85%) → contracted (60%) → narrow (15%)
- Motion behavior: Slow air layer drift from left to right over 30s in deepening, back in integration
- Depth behavior: Substrate centered (0.5m), air layer moves to rear (2.5m) in deepening, returns center

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Long breath (0.07-0.13 Hz) for substrate, slower body pulse (0.15-0.35 Hz) for harmonic bed
- Change rule: Breath rate slows by 0.01 Hz every 3 minutes; body pulse depth increases slightly in deepening

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1
- Reference pitch-contour profile: reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1 (84.5% falling_settling)
- Carrier instrument / layer: Soft cello patch (low-mid register, warm timbre)
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs (0.9-3.3s) with 0.5-1.8s rests
- Contour behavior: Falling/settling (84.5% of phrases) with slight rise in fractionation
- Weighting behavior: Slight sustain increase (0.2s) at phrase end for weighted emphasis
- Return behavior: Carrier returns with same contour but lower register (1/4 tone) in deepening/core
- Salience limit: Carrier at -12 dB relative to harmonic bed (audible but not foreground)

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Low-mid warmth bloom (0.5s soft swell in F2 drone)
- First pairing: At 0:45, after first carrier phrase resolves
- Withholding plan: Absent for 90 seconds (until induction threshold)
- Return / trigger plan: At 3:00 (induction), 7:30 (deepening stability), 15:00 (core reassurance), 21:00 (fractionation re-deepening), 22:30 (integration)
- Salience policy: Bloom at -18 dB relative to substrate (audible enough to learn, not salient)

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce carrier voice and substrate with sparse micro-bloom in orientation
- Variation rule: Carrier contour varies by stage; harmonic bed shifts to contrast color
- Contrast rule: Bb minor 9th introduced in induction for gentle harmonic shift without dissonance
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif cell recurs transformed (register, width) in core/fractionation
- Foreground rule: Carrier voice is only foreground element; remains subsalient throughout
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld for 90 seconds after first pairing to preserve meaning
- Fractionation rule: Carrier becomes slightly brighter (10% upper partials) for 60s, returns darker over next 60s
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor bloom paired with carrier phrase settling (downward contour)
- Dissolution / cadence rule: End with slow width contraction to 15% and air layer fade-out
- Anti-padding rule: No events repeated merely to fill time; each micro-event has purpose

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: F2 substrate with slow beating
- Harmonic color function: Harmonic bed carrying home/contrast colors
- Motion function: Carrier voice providing continuous prosody
- Foreground / event function: Primary motif cell (rarely)
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Carrier voice with weighted cadence
- Anchor / trigger function: Low-mid warmth bloom in substrate
- Air / bloom function: High-frequency air layer opening/closing
- Spatial / depth function: Air layer movement and width arc
- Silence / restraint function: Anchor withholding for 90 seconds

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One micro-bloom every 20-45 seconds
- Scene-change rate: Seven stages with soft transitions (no abrupt changes)
- Long-wave change: Harmonic bed shifts from home to contrast color and back
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without meaningful change (covered by micro-events)
- Repetition policy: Primary motif recurs transformed, not identical
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:30-21:30, re-deepening 21:30-22:00
- Anchor recurrence spacing: 0:45, 3:00, 7:30, 15:00, 21:00, 22:30

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: stereo
- Spatial arc: Narrow (10%) → wide (85%) → contracted (60%) → narrow (15%)
- Layer placement principles: Substrate centered, harmonic bed wide/stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges/rear
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; spatial movement over 30 seconds minimum
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible and motion not alerting

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No perceptible change for >45 seconds in any stage
  - Revision target: Add micro-bloom or harmonic shift to maintain continuity

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Primary motif appears once and vanishes without transformation
  - Revision target: Transform motif by register, width, or filtering in core/fractionation

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like reset or new section, not deeper return
  - Revision target: Strengthen re-deepening through darker register and wider spatial return

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor cues absent, random, or notification-like
  - Revision target: Soften onset to -18 dB and ensure pairing with carrier settling

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: No clear depth progression or stage function mapping
  - Revision target: Map all harmonic shifts, spatial changes, and carrier contour to stage functions

- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: Piece simply fades out without dissolution criteria
  - Revision target: Add width contraction to 15% and air layer fade-out before final substrate
