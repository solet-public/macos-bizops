## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A 24-minute music-only self-hypnosis journey guiding attention from external orientation to deep internal absorption and gentle return
- Musical problem: Creating a continuous, non-intrusive vocal-styled instrumental carrier that guides through seven hypnotic stages without becoming a foreground melody
- Listener stance: Following the continuous lead with relaxed attention, allowing absorption to deepen without active monitoring
- What this is not: Dramatic, sad narrative, triumphant, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension
- Style-family fit: Long-Form Hypnotic Journey with Prosodic Lead (matches Work Manifest's style family)

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A continuous breath-length instrumental carrier guides the listener through seven hypnotic stages using a falling-settling motif and evolving harmonic bed, with subtle nonverbal anchors reinforcing safe absorption
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish → deepen → fractionate (brief lightening) → return
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, deep ocean current carrying the listener through layers of water without visible surface disturbance
- Musical consequence: The falling-settling motif becomes the carrier's identity; harmonic bed shifts from F2 open voicings to Db shadow color and back, with spatial width expanding then contracting; carrier's register lowers as depth increases
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows for gradual deepening (6 min), sustained core work (7 min), and fractionation pocket requiring time to feel like natural lightening without reset

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: music-only self-hypnosis for focused immersive listening
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 3:00, baseline-to-light, settle and establish safety
  - Induction / Narrowing: 4:00, light-to-medium, pace and lead attention inward
  - Deepening: 6:00, medium-to-deep, reduce orienting load and widen space
  - Core Absorptive Work: 7:00, deep, sustain absorption with micro-developments
  - Fractionation Pocket: 2:00, deep-to-light-medium-to-deep, briefly lighten then return deeper
  - Integration: 1:30, medium-to-light, stabilize and reduce depth pressure
  - Return / Reorientation: 0:30, light-to-baseline, reorient without abrupt arousal
- Depth trajectory: Baseline (orientation) → light (induction) → medium (deepening start) → deep (core work) → brief light-medium (fractionation) → medium-light (integration) → baseline (return). Depth increases via lower register, darker harmonic color, wider spatial field; decreases via higher register (fractionation lightening), brighter upper partials, narrower width
- Pacing-and-leading plan: First pace broad listening with slow ground and sparse carrier (orientation), then lengthen rests, lower register, darken brightness, widen space to narrow attention
- Ratification plan: Carrier's downward settling contour at phrase end paired with warmth bloom anchor becomes learned cue for safe absorption
- Suggestion-compatible windows: Carrier's permissive pauses (0.5-1.8s) and anchor's soft bloom
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, alerting cues, or sudden brightness spikes

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Three-note falling-settling cell (C3-Ab2-G2) with 0.5s rest after G2
- First form: Soft carrier phrase (1.2s duration) in orientation with breath-shaped envelope
- Later forms: Deepening (B2-G2-Eb2, longer decay), Core (Ab2-G2, slower phrasing), Integration (C3-Ab2-G2 at higher register)

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F2 root with open C (F-A-C) and Eb warmth (Ebmaj7), G add9
- Contrast color: Db root with common-tone F retained (Db-F-Ab-Bb), luminous shadow
- Return color: F2 root with fewer tones (open C and Eb warmth), softened upper partials, narrower spatial field

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Warm F2 drone with slow beating (0.1-0.3 Hz), long attack/release
- Event layer behavior: Rare submerged motif fragments (1-2 per section), soft partial blooms at anchor returns
- Noise / air behavior: Filtered high air (10-20 kHz) at edges with slow opening/closing (15s cycles)

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow → medium-wide (deepening) → narrow (return)
- Motion behavior: Carrier near center with low motion; air layer drifts slowly from edges to rear
- Depth behavior: Deepening uses wider field and lower register; core work has intimate depth (less reverb); return contracts to center

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow incommensurable rates (0.05-0.2 Hz) for ground/harmonic bed; asymmetric shimmer in air layer
- Change rule: Modulation rates slow as depth increases; fractionation lightening uses slightly faster air shimmer

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: v1 empirical study (6-14s phrase arcs, 0.5-1.8s rests)
- Reference pitch-contour profile: v1 (falling_settling dominant, 84.5%)
- Carrier instrument / layer: Sarangi-like bowed string (warm, low-mid, breathy)
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs with permissive pauses; downward settling at deepening
- Contour behavior: Falling_settling primary, brief rising for fractionation lightening (1-2% of phrases)
- Weighting behavior: Slight emphasis on Ab2 in motif cell, soft attack
- Return behavior: Transformed motif returns at deepening stability and integration
- Salience limit: 6dB above harmonic bed, 10dB above substrate; never becomes lead melody

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny low-mid warmth bloom (F2-Ab2, 0.5s duration)
- First pairing: Orientation at 0:45 (after first carrier phrase resolves)
- Withholding plan: Absent for ≥90s after first pairing (until induction threshold)
- Return / trigger plan: 3:45 (induction), 10:30 (deepening stability), 16:45 (core reassurance), 21:30 (fractionation re-deepening), 22:45 (integration)
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn (0.5dB above bed), not salient enough to monitor

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce ground, harmonic bed, and carrier with single motif cell in orientation
- Variation rule: Transform motif by register, density, spatial placement per stage; harmonic bed shifts between home/contrast colors
- Contrast rule: Shift to Db contrast color during deepening, return to home for integration
- Recurrence rule: Motif cell returns transformed in each stage; anchor cue returns at depth thresholds
- Foreground rule: Carrier is foreground throughout (6dB+ above bed); no other events become foreground
- Withholding rule: Anchor cue withheld for 90s after first pairing; event layer withheld in induction
- Fractionation rule: Lightening via brighter upper partials (20:00-21:00) and faster air shimmer, re-deepening via darker harmonic bed (21:00-22:00) without reset
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier release in orientation; returns at depth thresholds to reinforce safe absorption
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final fade uses harmonic simplification (home color to F2 root only) and spatial contraction
- Anti-padding rule: No looped events; all changes motivated by stage progression

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: F2 drone (warm, slow beating)
- Harmonic color function: harmonic_body layer (F-centered open voicings, Db contrast)
- Motion function: carrier_layer and air_bloom layer
- Foreground / event function: carrier_layer (only foreground element)
- Voice-adjacent induction function: sarangi-like carrier with breath-length phrases
- Anchor / trigger function: warmth bloom at carrier release points
- Air / bloom function: filtered high air (slow opening/closing)
- Spatial / depth function: stereo width and layer placement
- Silence / restraint function: withholding of event_layer during induction

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One motif fragment or anchor cue every 15-45 seconds
- Scene-change rate: Harmonic region shifts every 75-150 seconds (home→Db at 7:00, Db→home at 20:00)
- Long-wave change: Harmonic bed shifts and spatial width arc
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without meaningful change (e.g., harmonic bed shift at 7:00)
- Repetition policy: Motif cell returns transformed; no event repetition without transformation
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00
- Anchor recurrence spacing: 0:45, 3:45, 10:30, 16:45, 21:30, 22:45

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: stereo
- Spatial arc: narrow (0-3:00) → medium-wide (7:00-13:00) → narrow (23:30-24:00)
- Layer placement principles: ground centered, harmonic_body wide stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges and rear
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; spatial motion over 20-120 seconds allowed for air layer
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible and motion not alerting

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No perceptible change for >45 seconds (e.g., harmonic bed unchanged in core work)
  - Revision target: Add micro-event or shift to contrast color

- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: Could be any ambient piece; no specific motif or harmonic signature
  - Revision target: Add falling-settling motif and Db contrast color

- Failure mode: too busy
  - Evidence: More than one event per 15 seconds (e.g., multiple motif fragments)
  - Revision target: Reduce event rate to one per 30 seconds

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Motif cell appears once and vanishes; anchor returns identically
  - Revision target: Transform motif (e.g., lower register in deepening) and vary anchor

- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: Fades out without harmonic simplification or spatial contraction
  - Revision target: Add F2 root only and contract to narrow

- Failure mode: weak spatial composition
  - Evidence: Output mono or width doesn't change
  - Revision target: Implement stereo construction with specified arc

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: No clear stage progression; depth doesn't change
  - Revision target: Map stages to concrete parameters (register, brightness, width)

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like reset (bright and fast)
  - Revision target: Make lightening brief with brighter upper partials, re-deepen via darker harmonic bed

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor too obvious (3dB above bed) or random
  - Revision target: Soften to 0.5dB above bed and pair with carrier release

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier too bright or mechanical
  - Revision target: Use sarangi-like timbre with breath-shaped envelope and falling_settling contour
