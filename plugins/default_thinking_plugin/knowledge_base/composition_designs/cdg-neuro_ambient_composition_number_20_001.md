## Artistic Reading
- User image / premise: A 24-minute music-only self-hypnosis journey guiding attention from external orientation to protected internal absorption and back.
- Musical problem: Creating continuous non-intrusive musical environment that supports deep absorption without vocal cues or rhythmic patterns.
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening, allowing attention to settle without monitoring the music itself.
- What this is not: Dramatic, sad narrative, triumphant, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension.
- Style-family fit: Structured Hypnotic Architecture (Carbon Based Lifeforms/Solar Fields tradition) with drone-sustained tone, body resonance, generative systems.

## Chosen Musical Premise
- Premise sentence: A continuous field of warm low-mid resonance evolves through seven hypnotic stages using a falling-settling motif and nonverbal anchors to guide attention inward without interruption.
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish, deepen, fractionate (briefly lighten and return deeper), stabilize, dissolve.
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow-moving river of warm light that deepens into a calm pool, briefly opens to the surface for air before returning deeper.
- Musical consequence: River depth (register/density) and width (stereo spread) change to mirror hypnotic journey; spectral brightness recedes during deepening and returns gently in integration.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows full seven-stage progression with sufficient time for anchors to become learned without rushing.

## Hypnotic Function Architecture
- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and reorientation.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 3:00, baseline-to-light, establish safety and first carrier pairing.
  - Induction / Narrowing: 4:00, light-to-medium, pace attention inward with carrier variants.
  - Deepening: 6:00, medium-to-deep, widen space and lower salience to reduce orienting load.
  - Core Absorptive Work: 7:00, deep, sustain absorption with rare carrier returns and anchor reassurance.
  - Fractionation Pocket: 2:00, deep-to-light-medium-to-deep, briefly lighten then return deeper without reset.
  - Integration / Stabilization: 1:30, medium-to-light, return familiar material in calmer form.
  - Return / Reorientation: 0:30, light-to-baseline, dissolve and reorient attention outward.
- Depth trajectory: Baseline → light (settled) → medium (narrowed attention) → deep (absorbed) → light-medium (fractionation opening) → deep (re-deepened) → medium-light (stabilized) → baseline. Achieved via register descent, density reduction, spectral darkening during deepening; brightness increase and spatial widening during fractionation lightening.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Start with broad slow ground and sparse carrier; lengthen rests, lower register, darken brightness, widen space to guide attention inward.
- Ratification plan: Nonverbal anchors (warmth blooms) paired with carrier releases at key depth thresholds to confirm state without words.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: 7:30-9:00 (deepening), 13:00-18:30 (core work), 22:00-23:00 (integration).
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, or sudden brightness spikes; all transitions soft crossfades over 3-5 seconds.

## Material Inventory
### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Three-note falling-settling cell (C3-Ab2-G2) with 0.5s interval between notes.
- First form: Soft carrier phrase (12s duration) during orientation after ground established.
- Later forms: Lower-slower-deepening form (slower tempo, lower register) during deepening; thinned-core-fragment (reduced density, filtered to lower partials) during core work; clearer-integration-return (slightly brighter contour) during integration.

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F2 root with open C (F-C), Eb warmth (F-Eb), G add9 (F-G-Bb-D) — low register open fifths and octaves.
- Contrast color: Db2 root (Db-Fb) with common-tone F retained, creating luminous shadow during fractionation.
- Return color: F2 root with fewer tones (only open C and Eb), softened upper partials, narrower spatial field.

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Ground drone with slow beating (0.1 Hz) and long attack (8-45s), stable field.
- Event layer behavior: Rare motif fragments (1 per 30s) and soft partial blooms, appearing only during deepening/core work.
- Noise / air behavior: Filtered high air (above 2400 Hz) at edges with slow opening/closing (10-60s), subtle shimmer.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (0:00-3:00) → medium (3:00-7:00) → wide (7:00-13:00) → medium-wide (13:00-20:00) → narrow (20:00-24:00).
- Motion behavior: Slow drift of air layer from rear to edges; anchor cues localized but soft.
- Depth behavior: Near (orientation) → medium (induction) → deep (deepening/core work) → near (integration/return).

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath (0.07-0.13 Hz) for ground drone; asymmetric shimmer (multiple incommensurable rates: 0.15-0.35 Hz, 0.2-0.4 Hz) for harmonic body.
- Change rule: Modulation rates slow and deepen during deepening; become more irregular in core work to avoid predictability.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: `reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1`.
- Reference pitch-contour profile: `reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1` (falling_settling dominant).
- Carrier instrument / layer: Sarangi-like bowed string patch, low-mid register (C2-G3).
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs (8-16s) with soft attacks and long releases; downward settling at deepening points.
- Contour behavior: Falling_settling primary, brief opening for fractionation (arch contour).
- Weighting behavior: Emphasis via slight register warmth and spatial widening at phrase ends.
- Return behavior: Returns transformed (lower, slower) to ratify depth and guide fractionation return.
- Salience limit: Carrier volume at -15 dB, never exceeding 20% of field's dynamic range.

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny low-mid warmth bloom (30ms, 87.31 Hz + minor third) paired with carrier release.
- First pairing: Orientation (after first carrier phrase resolves at 1:30), paired with subtle field widening.
- Withholding plan: Absent for 90 seconds after first pairing (until induction threshold).
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at induction threshold (3:00), deepening stability point (7:00), core reassurance (13:00), fractionation re-deepening (21:00), integration (22:30).
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn (-18 dB) but not salient enough to monitor (never loudest element).

## Development Grammar
- Establishment rule: Introduce ground drone and first carrier phrase with minimal event density.
- Variation rule: Material changes via register descent, spectral darkening, spatial widening during deepening.
- Contrast rule: Use Db contrast color only in fractionation pocket; no harmonic reset during integration.
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transformed (not identical) at each major depth threshold.
- Foreground rule: Event layer remains sparse (1 per 30s) and never becomes a lead.
- Withholding rule: Anchor absent for 90 seconds after first pairing; carrier mostly absent during core work.
- Fractionation rule: Brief lightening (20% amplitude increase in brightness) from 20:00-21:00, then re-deepening (darkening and widening) from 21:00-22:00.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier release at comfort points to build learned association.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final fade must include harmonic simplification (return color) and spatial contraction.
- Anti-padding rule: No material looped merely to fill time; all events have purpose.

## Orchestration Map
- Root / ground function: F2 drone with slow beating (0.1 Hz), centered.
- Harmonic color function: Dark low-mid pad using F-centered open voicings, wide-stable.
- Motion function: Carrier layer with breath-length phrase arcs (8-16s), near-center.
- Foreground / event function: Rare motif fragments and soft partial blooms (1 per 30s).
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Sarangi-like carrier with downward settling, -15 dB.
- Anchor / trigger function: Low-mid warmth bloom (30ms) paired with carrier release.
- Air / bloom function: Filtered high air at edges (2400 Hz cutoff), slow opening/closing.
- Spatial / depth function: Width arc (narrow → wide → narrow) with air layer motion.
- Silence / restraint function: Carrier absent during core work; anchor withheld for 90s.

## Temporal Behavior
- Micro-event rate: One motif fragment or partial bloom every 15-45 seconds.
- Scene-change rate: Every 75-150 seconds (harmonic region shifts).
- Long-wave change: Spectral darkening during deepening, brightness increase in fractionation.
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without meaningful change or withholding.
- Repetition policy: Motif returns transformed; no identical loops.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: First pairing (1:30), then returns at 3:00, 7:00, 13:00, 21:00, 22:30.

## Spatial Composition
- Stereo requirement: Stereo.
- Spatial arc: Narrow (0:00) → medium (3:00) → wide (7:00) → medium-wide (13:00) → narrow (20:00).
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered, harmonic body wide-stable, carrier near-center low-motion, air at edges/rear.
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; spatial movement limited to 20-120s for anchor gestures.
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible, motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria
- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No perceptible change for >45s in core work.
  - Revision target: Add micro-event (motif fragment) at 15s intervals.

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Motif appears once and vanishes.
  - Revision target: Transform motif by register (lower) for deepening return.

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like reset, not deeper return.
  - Revision target: Bind re-deepening to anchor return at 21:00.

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier behaves like lead melody.
  - Revision target: Reduce carrier volume to -15 dB, add permissive pauses.

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: No clear depth trajectory.
  - Revision target: Add spectral darkening (2400 Hz cutoff) during deepening.
