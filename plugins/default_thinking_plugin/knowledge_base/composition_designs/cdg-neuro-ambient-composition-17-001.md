## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A 24-minute self-hypnosis journey using structured ambient development to guide attention through absorption and return without vocal content.
- Musical problem: How to create continuous, non-intrusive progression that deepens without escalation while using subtle musical cues for trance architecture.
- Listener stance: Inward, non-monitoring deep listening; attention flows from environmental to internal without effort.
- What this is not: Pop song, cinematic tension bed, or dramatic narrative; no hooks, percussion, or abrupt changes.
- Style-family fit: Structured Hypnotic Architecture (staged form with voice-adjacent carrier) from neuro-ambient style family.

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A continuous harmonic field evolves through seven hypnotic stages using a voice-adjacent carrier to guide attention deeper while maintaining unbroken ambient flow.
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish → deepen → fractionate (lighten/deeper return) → integrate → dissolve.
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, deepening ocean current carrying the listener through water layers without breaking surface.
- Musical consequence: Harmonic regions shift slowly (30s transitions) with density/spatial width changes; carrier phrases settle downward to mark depth thresholds.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows seven distinct stages with sufficient time for each hypnotic phase to establish without rushing.

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and return.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation (3:00): Baseline to light; establish carrier and first anchor pairing.
  - Induction (4:00): Light to medium; pace attention with carrier variants and anchor return.
  - Deepening (6:00): Medium to deep; widen space, lower carrier salience, stabilize root memory.
  - Core Absorptive Work (7:00): Deep; sustain with rare carrier returns and motif fragments.
  - Fractionation Pocket (2:00): Deep to light-medium to deep; brief brightness then deeper return.
  - Integration (1:30): Medium to light; clear carrier and harmonic simplification.
  - Return (0:30): Light to baseline; dissolve with air fade and ground residue.
- Depth trajectory: Gradual harmonic density increase (0.5 to 1.2) and spatial widening (narrow to wide) from orientation to deepening, then gradual thinning/narrowing for return.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Carrier phrases lengthen (1.5s to 3.0s) and settle downward during induction, then deepen with slower phrasing.
- Ratification plan: Carrier returns at depth thresholds (induction, deepening stability) with transformed contour to confirm state.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: During Core Absorptive Work and Integration, when carrier is sparse but present.
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt harmonic shifts; all transitions use 30s crossfades with common tones.

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Descending minor third (A3-G#3) with 0.5s delay between notes.
- First form: Introduced in Core Absorptive Work as single sparse event at 13:00.
- Later forms: Transposed down an octave (A2-G#2) in Integration; filtered and widened in Fractionation Pocket.

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F3 root with open fifth (C4) and minor seventh (Eb4) in low register.
- Contrast color: Db3 root with major third (F#4) and fifth (Ab4), retaining F2 anchor.
- Return color: Home color with Eb4 omitted and C4 softened to open fifth.

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Ground drone with slow beating (0.1 Hz) and long attack (25s).
- Event layer behavior: Rare motif fragments (every 45s) and soft partial blooms (every 120s).
- Noise / air behavior: Air layer opens slowly at edges, attack 30s and release 60s.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (15°) → medium (45°) → wide (90°) → medium-wide (75°) → narrow (20°).
- Motion behavior: Slow drift left to right in air layer; anchor stays local but soft.
- Depth behavior: Ground centered, harmonic body wide and stable, carrier near center.

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath (0.1 Hz) for ground, asymmetric shimmer (multiple rates) for air.
- Change rule: Modulation depth decreases by 10% per stage; rates become less regular.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1.
- Carrier instrument / layer: Bass clarinet patch (warm low-mid, filtered to 100-300 Hz).
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs (1.5s to 3.0s) with soft attack and long release.
- Contour behavior: Downward settling primary; brief opening during Fractionation Pocket.
- Weighting behavior: Emphasis on phrase endings via slight register warmth and spatial widening.
- Return behavior: Carrier returns transformed (transposed, filtered) at depth thresholds.
- Salience limit: Max 0.5s per phrase; never above -18 dB.

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny low-mid warmth bloom (F2-F3) at carrier release.
- First pairing: Orientation, after first carrier phrase resolves (at 1:45).
- Withholding plan: Absent for 90s after first pairing (until induction threshold at 3:00).
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at induction, deepening stability (7:00), core reassurance (16:30), fractionation re-deepening (21:00), integration.
- Salience policy: Audible at -25 dB, not monitorable.

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce ground drone and carrier seed in Orientation.
- Variation rule: Harmonic region shifts (home → contrast) with 30s crossfades.
- Contrast rule: Brightness and density increase during Fractionation Pocket, then return to dark.
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transposed and filtered in Integration; carrier returns transformed at thresholds.
- Foreground rule: Event layer only during Core Absorptive Work and Fractionation Pocket (max 1 event/45s).
- Withholding rule: Anchor absent for 90s after first pairing; motif withheld until Core Absorptive Work.
- Fractionation rule: Lightening (20:00-21:00) with brighter air and clearer carrier; re-deepening (21:00-22:00) with darker harmonic body and wider carrier.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier release; withheld to maintain meaning.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final dissolve requires harmonic simplification (return color) and spatial contraction.
- Anti-padding rule: No event loops; all events sparse and transformative.

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: Ground drone (F2) with slow beating.
- Harmonic color function: Harmonic body layer (home/contrast colors).
- Motion function: Air layer with slow drift and modulation.
- Foreground / event function: Primary motif fragment (Core, Fractionation).
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Bass clarinet carrier layer.
- Anchor / trigger function: Low-mid warmth bloom (paired with carrier release).
- Air / bloom function: Filtered high air at edges.
- Spatial / depth function: Stereo width and layer placement (ground centered, harmonic body wide).
- Silence / restraint function: Withholding of motif and anchor until designated stages.

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One event every 15-45 seconds (motif fragment, partial bloom).
- Scene-change rate: Every 75-150 seconds (harmonic region shifts, density changes).
- Long-wave change: Harmonic color shift from home to contrast (Core Absorptive Work), then return.
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without deliberate change (e.g., air layer opening).
- Repetition policy: Events sparse and transformative; no wallpaper repetition.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: First at 3:00, then at 7:00, 16:30, 21:00, 23:00.

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: Stereo.
- Spatial arc: Narrow (15°) → medium (30°) → wide (60°) → medium-wide (75°) → narrow (20°).
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered, harmonic body wide and stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges.
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; slow drift (10s cycle) for air layer.
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible and motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No harmonic or spatial change for >45s.
  - Revision target: Add micro-event (e.g., air layer opening) or density shift.

- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: Harmonic signature indistinct from other neuro-ambient pieces.
  - Revision target: Define specific harmonic intervals (e.g., F3-C4-Eb4) and contrast (Db3-F#4-Ab4).

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Primary motif appears once and vanishes.
  - Revision target: Return motif transposed in Integration.

- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: Piece fades without harmonic simplification.
  - Revision target: Add return color and spatial contraction.

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Fractionation pocket feels like new section.
  - Revision target: Bind lightening to carrier clarity and re-deepening to darker harmonic body.

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor cue too obvious (e.g., at -15 dB).
  - Revision target: Reduce anchor to -25 dB and add withholding.

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier phrases metrical (e.g., every 4s).
  - Revision target: Use breath-length phrases (1.5-3.0s) with soft attack.
