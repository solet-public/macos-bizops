## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: Slow descent into resonant inner space with subtle rhythmic suggestion through delay-processed arpeggios and breath-like carrier cadences
- Musical problem: Creating deep absorption without rhythmic pulses or transient attacks while maintaining continuous harmonic evolution for trance progression
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening with attention focused on field continuity rather than discrete events
- What this is not: Dramatic, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension, or hard-percussive groove
- Style-family fit: Sequenced Ambient (Carbon Based Lifeforms/Solar Fields) with harmonic region progression and breath-length phrasing

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A 24-minute neuro-ambient composition guiding listeners through structured depth stages via harmonic region shifts, breath-like carrier cadences, and spectral evolution with a stable F2 anchor.
- Primary dramatic motion: Deepen → sustain → fractionate → stabilize → return
- Sound-world metaphor: Descending into a resonant cave where sound walls gradually close in, then gently open during fractionation
- Musical consequence: Harmonic shifts every 1.5-2 minutes (root changes), spectral centroid lowering from 1500Hz to 400Hz, spatial widening to medium-wide during deepening
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes earned through seven-stage hypnotic architecture with fractionation renewal cycles preventing habituation

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and gentle return
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 3:00, baseline-to-light, establish safety
  - Induction / Narrowing: 4:00, light-to-medium, pace attention inward
  - Deepening: 6:00, medium-to-deep, reduce orienting load
  - Core Absorptive Work: 7:00, deep, sustain absorption
  - Fractionation Pocket: 2:00, deep-to-light-medium-to-deep, brief lightening then deeper return
  - Integration / Stabilization: 1:30, medium-to-light, stabilize calm
  - Return / Reorientation: 0:30, light-to-baseline, reorient gently
- Depth trajectory: Level 0 (baseline) → Level 1 (light) at 3:00 → Level 2 (medium) at 7:00 → Level 3 (deep) at 13:00 → Level 2.5 during fractionation → Level 2 (stabilized) at 23:30
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Induction uses harmonic shifts every 1.5 minutes to pace attention inward; deepening introduces carrier phrases with downward settling
- Ratification plan: Anchor bloom (warm spectral opening) paired with first carrier phrase in orientation, then returned at deepening threshold
- Suggestion-compatible windows: Fractionation pocket (20:00-21:00) with no foreground events
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt changes; all transitions crossfade ≥12 seconds with spectral darkening during deepening

## Material Inventory

- Primary motif / gesture:
  - Identity: Minor third interval (3 semitones) with upward glide followed by downward settling
  - First form: Submerged glint at 2000Hz (amplitude -18dB) during orientation
  - Later forms: Transposed downward, filtered darker (lowpass at 2500Hz), spatially shifted

- Harmonic signature:
  - Home color: F2 minor 7th (root=87.31Hz, intervals=[0,3,7,10])
  - Contrast color: Bb2 minor 9th (root=116.54Hz, intervals=[0,3,7,10,14])
  - Return color: F2 minor 7th with omitted fifth (intervals=[0,3,10])

- Texture family:
  - Continuous layer: Harmonic bed with slow amplitude modulation (0.1Hz)
  - Event layer: Primary motif appearing every 25-45 seconds
  - Noise / air: Filtered high-air (lowpass at 3200Hz) with slow spectral opening

- Spatial material:
  - Width behavior: Narrow (centered anchor) → medium-wide (induction) → wide (core)
  - Motion behavior: Air layer drifts slowly (autopan at 0.05Hz)
  - Depth behavior: Reverb wet ratio increases from 0.3 (orientation) to 0.7 (core)

- Modulation identity:
  - Rate family: Slow breathing (0.1Hz) for bed, geological drift (0.05Hz) for anchor
  - Change rule: Modulation depth decreases during deepening to support deeper trance

- Hypnotist-voice analog:
  - Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1
  - Reference pitch-contour profile: falling_settling
  - Carrier instrument / layer: Warm low-mid reed pad (program 89, fluidr3_gm)
  - Cadence behavior: 4-8s phrases with permissive pauses (0.5-1.2s)
  - Contour behavior: Downward settling phrases
  - Weighting behavior: Longer, deeper phrases during deepening
  - Return behavior: Carrier returns transformed (lower register) at depth thresholds
  - Salience limit: Never exceeds -12dB relative to harmonic bed

- Nonverbal anchors and subtle triggers:
  - Anchor identity: Warm spectral bloom (2000Hz, 5ms duration)
  - First pairing: Bloom at end of first carrier phrase (orientation)
  - Withholding plan: Anchor absent during induction and deepening
  - Return / trigger plan: Bloom returns at end of deepening (13:00) and fractionation return
  - Salience policy: Bloom audible but not foregrounded (amplitude -18dB)

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Anchor and harmonic bed introduced without carrier or motif
- Variation rule: Harmonic bed shifts to Bb2 minor 9th during deepening with same voicing
- Contrast rule: Brighter harmonic color (Bb2) for contrast, then returns to home
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transformed (register shift) in each stage
- Foreground rule: Motif only foregrounded during fractionation lightening (20:00-21:00)
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld during induction and deepening
- Fractionation rule: Lighten via lowpass cutoff raise (3200Hz→4000Hz), return via cutoff drop (4000Hz→2500Hz)
- Trigger conditioning rule: Bloom paired with downward carrier phrase in orientation
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final carrier phrase settles to F2 with anchor fade-out
- Anti-padding rule: No identical event repetition; all events spaced ≥15s apart

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: Primary motif every 25-45 seconds
- Scene-change rate: Harmonic region shifts every 1.5-2 minutes
- Long-wave change: Spectral centroid lowering from 1500Hz to 400Hz
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without meaningful change
- Repetition policy: Events transformed on recurrence (no identical repetition)
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00
- Anchor recurrence spacing: Returns at 13:00 and 21:45

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: Stereo
- Spatial arc: Narrow → medium → wide → medium-wide → narrow (fractionation) → medium
- Layer placement principles: Anchor centered, harmonic bed wide stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges
- Motion limits: Air layer autopan max 0.2Hz (no alerting motion)
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible, motion not alerting

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No harmonic shifts or motif recurrence for >90 seconds
  - Revision target: Add harmonic region shift every 1.5 minutes and motif recurrence

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Motif appears once then vanishes without transformation
  - Revision target: Transform motif by register shift and filtering on each recurrence

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like reset, return not deeper
  - Revision target: Bind brightness to fractionation lightening (lowpass at 4000Hz), strengthen re-deepening via spectral darkening

- Failure mode: weak anchors
  - Evidence: Bloom cue absent, random, or too obvious
  - Revision target: Pair bloom with downward carrier phrase in orientation; withhold until deepening threshold

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: No depth trajectory, stage transitions feel abrupt
  - Revision target: Enforce spectral darkening during deepening and fractionation return
