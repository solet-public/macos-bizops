## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A continuous prosodic lead voice guiding listeners through a self-hypnosis journey without words, using instrumental carrier to create absorption.
- Musical problem: How to make harmonic continuity and vocal prosody feel like a single organic process without rhythmic or melodic hooks.
- Listener stance: Following the carrier's breath-length phrases as a safe, non-demanding anchor for attention.
- What this is not: Pop song with vocal hook, cinematic soundtrack, or rhythmic trance track.
- Style-family fit: Structured self-hypnosis journey with foreground_vocal_led_prosody mode, warm low-mid anchor.

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A sarangi-like carrier delivers continuous breath-length phrases that deepen through harmonic shifts and spatial widening, with subtle anchors marking depth thresholds.
- Primary dramatic motion: Deepening through harmonic color shifts and spatial expansion, with fractionation as a brief lightening before deeper return.
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow-moving river bed with occasional warm eddies that deepen as the water widens.
- Musical consequence: Harmonic regions shift via interval stack changes (not root jumps), spatial width expands then contracts, carrier phrases lengthen and darken.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows for five harmonic transitions (home → contrast → return) with sufficient time to establish and transform the carrier's prosody.

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for focused immersion.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 0:00-3:00, baseline_to_light, establish world and first carrier pairing.
  - Induction / Narrowing: 3:00-7:00, light_to_medium, pace attention inward via carrier phrasing.
  - Deepening: 7:00-13:00, medium_to_deep, widen space and lower carrier register.
  - Core Absorptive Work: 13:00-20:00, deep, sustain depth with thinned carrier.
  - Fractionation Pocket: 20:00-22:00, deep_to_light_medium_to_deep, briefly lighten then return deeper.
  - Integration: 22:00-23:30, medium_to_light, return carrier in clearer form.
  - Return / Reorientation: 23:30-24:00, light_to_baseline, dissolve carrier into harmonic bed.
- Depth trajectory: Carrier register descends (C3 → Bb2), spectral brightness reduces (4700 Hz → 2600 Hz), spatial width expands then contracts.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: First pace with slow ground and sparse carrier, then lengthen rests, lower register, darken brightness.
- Ratification plan: Anchor cues return at depth thresholds to confirm stability without words.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: 7:30-9:00 (deepening), 13:00-18:30 (core work), 22:00-23:00 (integration).
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt brightness spikes, hard cuts, or new harmonic roots without common tones.

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Three-note falling-settling cell (C3-Ab2-G2) in 1.5s phrase arc.
- First form: Soft carrier phrase at 0:45, resolving to ground drone.
- Later forms: Lowered register (Bb2-G2-F#2) in deepening, thinned to two notes (Ab2-G2) in core, clearer return at 22:30.

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F root (87.31 Hz) with interval stack [0, 3, 10] (F-C-Ab), warm low-mid pad.
- Contrast color: Db root (146.83 Hz) with interval stack [0, 5, 9, 12] (Db-Ab-Bb-Eb), luminous shadow.
- Return color: F root with interval stack [0, 3] (F-C), softened upper partials.

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Ground drone with slow beating (0.1 Hz) and 25s attack.
- Event layer behavior: Motif fragments at 15-45s intervals, soft-edged and filtered.
- Noise / air behavior: Filtered high air (200-4500 Hz) opening/closing over 60s cycles.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (10°) → wide (120°) → medium-wide (90°) → narrow (30°).
- Motion behavior: Slow drift in air layer (30s cycle), anchor stays centered.
- Depth behavior: Near field (ground) → mid-field (harmonic bed) → rear air.

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Long breath (0.07-0.13 Hz) for ground, gentle body pulse (0.15-0.35 Hz) for harmonic bed.
- Change rule: Modulation rates slow and deepen as piece progresses.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: v1 empirical study (84.5% falling_settling, 6-14s phrases).
- Carrier instrument / layer: Sarangi-like bowed string patch.
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs with 1.5s inter-phrase pauses.
- Contour behavior: Falling_settling dominant, with arches for fractionation lightening.
- Weighting behavior: Slight register warmth at phrase ends, no hard attacks.
- Return behavior: Motif returns transformed (e.g., lower register) at depth thresholds.
- Salience limit: 6 dB above harmonic bed, 10 dB above substrate.

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny low-mid warmth bloom (10-25 Hz, 0.8s duration).
- First pairing: At 1:30 (after first carrier phrase resolves), paired with ground drone.
- Withholding plan: Absent for 90s after first pairing (until induction threshold).
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at 3:00 (induction), 7:00 (deepening stability), 13:00 (core reassurance), 20:00 (fractionation re-deepening), 22:30 (integration).
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn, not salient enough to monitor.

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce carrier and ground together; no leadless gaps.
- Variation rule: Transform motif by register, density, or filtering without new intervals.
- Contrast rule: Shift harmonic color via interval stack changes (not root jumps).
- Recurrence rule: Motif returns at depth thresholds, transformed by register or width.
- Foreground rule: Carrier remains ≥6 dB above bed; no other foreground events.
- Withholding rule: Anchor absent for 90s after first pairing; motif fragments sparse (15-45s).
- Fractionation rule: Lighten via brief carrier clarity and brightness (20:00-21:00), return deeper via darker register.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier phrase resolution; returns at depth thresholds.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final fade requires harmonic simplification (F-C) and spatial contraction.
- Anti-padding rule: No looped events; all changes tied to development grammar.

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: F2 drone (87.31 Hz) with slow beating.
- Harmonic color function: Interval stack [0, 3, 10] for home; [0, 5, 9, 12] for contrast.
- Motion function: Carrier phrases (6-14s) with downward settling contour.
- Foreground / event function: Motif fragments at 15-45s intervals (soft-edged).
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Sarangi-like carrier with breath-length phrases.
- Anchor / trigger function: Low-mid warmth bloom (10-25 Hz) at depth thresholds.
- Air / bloom function: Filtered high air (200-4500 Hz) opening/closing over 60s.
- Spatial / depth function: Width arc (10°→120°→30°) with air layer drift.
- Silence / restraint function: Anchor absent for 90s after first pairing.

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One motif fragment every 15-45 seconds.
- Scene-change rate: Harmonic region shifts every 75-150 seconds.
- Long-wave change: Spectral brightness reduction (4700 Hz → 2600 Hz).
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without deliberate change.
- Repetition policy: Motif returns transformed; no identical repeats.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:00, re-deepening 21:00-22:00.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: Returns at 3:00, 7:00, 13:00, 20:00, 22:30.

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: Stereo with distinct layer roles.
- Spatial arc: Narrow (10°) → wide (120°) → medium-wide (90°) → narrow (30°).
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered, harmonic bed wide stable, carrier near-center low motion.
- Motion limits: Air layer drift 30s cycle; no fast autopan.
- Verification obligation: Confirm not mono, width arc audible, motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No harmonic shift or carrier transformation for >45s.
  - Revision target: Add interval stack change at 10:00.

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Motif appears once and vanishes.
  - Revision target: Transform motif at deepening threshold (7:00).

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like reset; return not deeper.
  - Revision target: Reduce fractionation brightness to 3500 Hz; deepen return via register.

- Failure mode: weak anchors
  - Evidence: Cues absent or too obvious.
  - Revision target: Move anchor to carrier phrase resolution; soften onset.

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier phrases mechanical or too bright.
  - Revision target: Apply empirical contour (84.5% falling_settling); lower spectral brightness.
