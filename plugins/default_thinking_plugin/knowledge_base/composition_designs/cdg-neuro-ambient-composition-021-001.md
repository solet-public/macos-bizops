## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A deep, evolving journey through layered harmonic space guided by a breath-like instrumental carrier, evoking neural quietude and subtle internal reorganization
- Musical problem: Creating hypnotic depth progression without vocal elements while maintaining warm, slightly dark tonal posture and low event density
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening with attention shifting from environmental to internal absorption
- What this is not: Vocal narration, foreground melodies, rhythmic percussion, hard transients, abrupt transitions, notification-like cues
- Style-family fit: Sequenced Ambient (Carbon Based Lifeforms/Solar Fields) with structured hypnotic architecture

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A 24-minute harmonic journey guided by breath-length piano pad phrases that deepen absorption through weighted phrasing, spectral unsealing, and harmonic evolution without vocal elements
- Primary dramatic motion: Deepen (through harmonic shift, spatial expansion, and carrier transformation)
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow current of warm water carrying the listener through layers of sediment and light
- Musical consequence: Harmonic density increases then thins, register shifts from low to mid with air bloom, stereo width evolves from narrow to wide then contracts
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows full hypnotic architecture progression with sufficient time for each stage's depth trajectory (core absorptive work requires 7 minutes to sustain deep state)

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis bed for deep absorption and internal reorganization
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 3:00, baseline to light (establish world and first carrier pairing)
  - Induction / Narrowing: 4:00, light to medium (pace and lead attention through carrier phrasing)
  - Deepening: 6:00, medium to deep (widen space and stabilize root memory)
  - Core Absorptive Work: 7:00, deep (sustain absorption with microdevelopment)
  - Fractionation Pocket: 2:00, deep to light-medium to deep (briefly lighten then return deeper)
  - Integration / Stabilization: 1:30, medium to light (return familiar material in calmer form)
  - Return / Reorientation: 0:30, light to baseline (dissolve and reorient)
- Depth trajectory: Baseline → Light (orientation) → Medium (induction) → Deep (deepening/core) → Brief light-medium (fractionation) → Light (integration) → Baseline (return). Achieved through harmonic density, carrier salience, and stereo width.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Carrier phrases lengthen (1.5s → 2.3s) during induction, with permissive pauses (0.75s) to narrow attention.
- Ratification plan: Carrier returns with downward-settling contour at key depth points (deepening, core work) to ratify state.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: Quiet moments after carrier phrases (0.5-1.2s) where spoken guidance could sit without masking.
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt changes, hard transients, or sudden brightness spikes; all transitions follow natural weather shift principle.

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: 3-note interval cell (F4-A4-D5) with descending contour and soft glissando on D5
- First form: Introduced at 0:45 as single instance with soft attack and long release
- Later forms: Returns transposed down to E4-G4-C5 in deepening; fragmented with wider stereo placement in core work

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F2 root + A3 (minor third) + D4 (perfect fifth) → interval stack [0, 3, 7] at F2
- Contrast color: G#2 root + B3 (minor third) + E4 (perfect fifth) → interval stack [0, 3, 7] at G#2
- Return color: F2 + A3 (minor third) with D4 removed → open, less dense harmonic space

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: F2 substrate drone with slow amplitude modulation (0.1 Hz LFO)
- Event layer behavior: Micro-events every 15-45 seconds (single note bloom in air layer, 0.5-2s duration)
- Noise / air behavior: Pink noise high-passed at 2800 Hz with slow amplitude modulation (0.05 Hz)

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow → medium → wide → medium-wide → narrow (across stages)
- Motion behavior: Air layer moves slowly left-to-right over 60 seconds in deepening
- Depth behavior: Ground near (centered), harmonic body medium depth, air layer moves from rear to edges

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath (0.1 Hz for ground, 0.05 Hz for air) with asymmetric shimmer in harmonic bed
- Change rule: Ground modulation rate decreases (0.15 Hz → 0.07 Hz), air modulation increases (0.03 Hz → 0.12 Hz) over piece

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1
- Reference pitch-contour profile: falling_settling (from reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1)
- Carrier instrument / layer: GM program 89 (filtered piano pad) at -15 dB relative to harmonic bed
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrases (1.5-2.3s) with permissive pauses (0.75s), downward settling
- Contour behavior: Predominantly falling_settling, occasional scoop_then_settle for permissive gestures
- Weighting behavior: Slight sustain increase and warmth at phrase end without volume spike
- Return behavior: Returns with more submerged contour (lower register) and longer release in deepening
- Salience limit: Carrier remains conditionable without becoming attention-demanding

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: 50ms spectral unsealing (3.5kHz boost) during carrier release
- First pairing: At 0:45, anchor appears as carrier phrase ends (F4-A4-D5), creating settling sensation
- Withholding plan: Anchor absent during induction to build expectation before deepening return
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at 7:15 (deepening), 13:45 (core work), and 20:15 (fractionation return)
- Salience policy: Anchor at -25 dB – audible enough to learn, not salient enough to monitor

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce ground drone (F2), harmonic bed (F3), and carrier in orientation
- Variation rule: Harmonic bed shifts to G#2 during induction, A2 in deepening
- Contrast rule: Contrast color (G#2) introduced as harmonic shift, not key change
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transformed (transposed down) in deepening and core work
- Foreground rule: Event layer active only during fractionation (brief lightening)
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld until deepening to maintain meaning
- Fractionation rule: Lightening via air layer brightness increase (30s), then return to deeper state
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with carrier release in orientation, creating conditioned association
- Dissolution / cadence rule: End by reducing air layer, narrowing stereo width, fading ground drone
- Anti-padding rule: No material repeated merely to fill time; every event has purpose

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: F2 substrate drone (87.31 Hz)
- Harmonic color function: Interval stack [0,3,7] at F2 (home), shifting to G#2/A2/Bb2
- Motion function: Primary motif cell and anchor events
- Foreground / event function: Micro-events in air layer (fractionation only)
- Voice-adjacent induction function: GM 89 piano pad with breath-length phrasing
- Anchor / trigger function: Spectral unsealing (3.5kHz boost) at carrier release
- Air / bloom function: Pink noise high-passed at 2800 Hz with slow modulation
- Spatial / depth function: Stereo width arc and air layer movement
- Silence / restraint function: Anchor withheld during induction

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One air layer bloom every 15-45 seconds
- Scene-change rate: Harmonic shifts and width changes every 75-150 seconds
- Long-wave change: Harmonic progression from F2 → Bb2 over piece
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without meaningful change or withholding
- Repetition policy: Primary motif returns transformed, not identical
- Fractionation timing: Lightening at 20:15 (30s duration), return to deeper state by 20:45
- Anchor recurrence spacing: Every 6.5 minutes (7:15, 13:45, 20:15)

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: stereo
- Spatial arc: narrow → medium → wide → medium-wide → narrow (across stages)
- Layer placement principles:
  - Ground: centered
  - Harmonic body: wide stable (left-right)
  - Carrier: near center, low motion
  - Air: edges and rear (moves left-to-right in deepening)
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; air layer motion over 60 seconds
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible and motion not alerting

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: no harmonic shift, no micro-events for >45 seconds
  - Revision target: Add harmonic region change or micro-event

- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: uses common ambient tropes without specific material
  - Revision target: Replace generic harmonic bed with defined interval stack progression

- Failure mode: too busy
  - Evidence: more than one micro-event per minute, carrier becomes foreground
  - Revision target: Reduce event layer to one every 30 seconds; lower carrier volume

- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: primary motif appears once and vanishes
  - Revision target: Ensure motif returns in deepening (transposed) and core work (fragmented)

- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: fades out without dissolution
  - Revision target: Add reduction in air layer and narrowing of stereo width

- Failure mode: weak spatial composition
  - Evidence: output is mono or static width
  - Revision target: Implement defined spatial arc

- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: no clear stage progression, depth trajectory not audible
  - Revision target: Adjust harmonic shifts and carrier behavior to match stage depths

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: lightening feels like reset or new section
  - Revision target: Ensure fractionation uses only brief brightness and returns to deeper state

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: anchor too obvious or absent during key moments
  - Revision target: Adjust anchor to -25 dB and ensure pairing with carrier release

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: carrier has mechanical timing or bright attack
  - Revision target: Use reference cadence profile and soften attacks
