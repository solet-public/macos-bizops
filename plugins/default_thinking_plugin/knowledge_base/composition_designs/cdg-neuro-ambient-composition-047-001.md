## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A 24-minute self-hypnosis journey through seven structured stages without words, guiding attention from external orientation to internal absorption and back.
- Musical problem: How to create continuous depth progression without alerting the listener, using only sound and no verbal cues.
- Listener stance: Inward deep listening with attention shifting from broad environmental to internal absorption and back.
- What this is not: Dramatic, sad narrative, triumphant, pop-hook-based, cinematic tension.
- Style-family fit: Uses drone-sustained tone (ground), body resonance (harmonic bed), generative systems (modulation), micropolyphony texture (event layer), and soundscape environment. Avoids excluded styles.

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A continuous field of warm low-mid resonance deepens through harmonic shifts and spatial expansion, with a voice-adjacent carrier guiding attention inward while subtle anchors reinforce safe absorption.
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish → Reveal → Deepen → Sustain → Lighten/Return → Stabilize → Dissolve.
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, deepening ocean current that carries the listener inward through layers of water (harmonic regions) while a gentle, rhythmic pulse (carrier) marks safe passage.
- Musical consequence: Harmonic regions shift downward in pitch to deepen the field; spatial width expands then contracts to mirror attentional focus; carrier phrases settle downward with breath-length arcs.
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows for the slow harmonic progression (87.31 Hz → 123.47 Hz) and the necessary time for attention to settle into deep absorption (8:45 core work).

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis for deep absorption and safe return.
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 0:00–3:00, baseline to light depth (establish world and first carrier pairing)
  - Induction / Narrowing: 3:00–7:00, light to medium depth (pace and lead attention inward)
  - Deepening: 7:00–13:00, medium to deep depth (widen space and stabilize root memory)
  - Core Absorptive Work: 13:00–21:45, deep depth (sustain absorption with microdevelopment)
  - Fractionation Pocket: 21:45–22:00, deep to light-medium to deep (briefly lighten then return deeper)
  - Integration / Stabilization: 22:00–23:30, medium to light depth (return familiar material in calmer form)
  - Return / Reorientation: 23:30–24:00, light to baseline (dissolve and cadence)
- Depth trajectory: Gradual pitch descent in harmonic bed (87.31 Hz → 123.47 Hz) with increasing spatial width (narrow to wide) and decreasing event density, then brief lightening during fractionation before returning deeper.
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Start with broad listening (slow ground, sparse carrier), then lengthen rests and lower register to narrow attention.
- Ratification plan: Carrier phrases settle downward at depth thresholds (induction threshold, deepening stability) to confirm state.
- Suggestion-compatible windows: 7:30–9:00 (induction), 13:00–18:30 (core), 22:00–23:00 (integration).
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, alerting cues, or sudden brightness spikes.

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: Three-note falling-settling cell (C3-Ab2-G2) with a 0.5-second gap between notes.
- First form: Soft carrier phrase during orientation after ground is established (0:45–1:30).
- Later forms: Lowered and slowed in deepening (D2-Bb1-G1), thinned core fragment (E3-C#3-A2) in core, clearer integration return (C4-Ab3-F3).

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F root (87.31 Hz) with open C, Eb warmth, G add9 (interval stack [0, 3, 10] at F2).
- Contrast color: Db root (138.59 Hz) with common-tone F retained, luminous shadow color (interval stack [0, 2, 6] at Db).
- Return color: F root with fewer color tones (only C and G), softened upper partials, narrower spatial field.

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Ground drone with slow beating (0.1–0.3 Hz) and long attack/release.
- Event layer behavior: Rare submerged motif fragments (every 15–45 seconds) and soft partial blooms.
- Noise / air behavior: Filtered high air (below 3200 Hz) that opens slowly at edges with long attack.

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (0:00–3:00) → Medium (3:00–7:00) → Wide (7:00–13:00) → Medium-wide (13:00–21:45) → Narrow (21:45–24:00).
- Motion behavior: Slow drift of air layer (10–60 seconds cycle) and subtle spatial softening at anchor points.
- Depth behavior: Near (ground, carrier) to far (air layer), with depth contracting during return.

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow incommensurable rates (0.05–0.2 Hz) for ground and harmonic bed, asymmetric shimmer in air layer.
- Change rule: Modulation rates slow over time; depth of modulation decreases as piece deepens.

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: `reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1` (empirical).
- Reference pitch-contour profile: `reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1` (84.5% falling_settling).
- Carrier instrument / layer: Sarangi-like bowed string (low-mid register, warm timbre).
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrase arcs (0.9–3.3 seconds) with soft attack and long release.
- Contour behavior: Downward settling primary, brief opening for fractionation (21:45–21:50).
- Weighting behavior: Slight register warmth and harmonic reinforcement at phrase ends.
- Return behavior: Returns transformed (lowered, slowed) at depth thresholds (induction threshold, deepening stability).
- Salience limit: Below lead melody attention; carrier level 6 dB above harmonic bed, 10 dB above substrate.

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Tiny low-mid warmth bloom (0.1–0.8 s) paired with carrier release.
- First pairing: Orientation at 2:15 (after first carrier phrase resolves).
- Withholding plan: Absent for at least 90 seconds after first pairing (until induction threshold).
- Return / trigger plan: Returns at induction threshold (4:30), deepening stability point (10:00), core reassurance (17:30), fractionation re-deepening (21:55), integration (22:45).
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn, not salient enough to monitor (level -15 dB relative to carrier).

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce ground, harmonic bed, and first carrier phrase with sparse events.
- Variation rule: Shift harmonic regions downward (87.31 → 92.50 Hz) and widen spatial field.
- Contrast rule: Brief lightening (fractionation) with brighter upper partials, then return to darker state.
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transformed (lowered register) at each depth threshold.
- Foreground rule: Event layer remains rare and submerged (one emergence per 60+ seconds).
- Withholding rule: Keep fractionation brightness and clear motif fragments withheld until core.
- Fractionation rule: Lightening (21:45–21:50) via brief brightness increase and carrier clarity, then re-deepening (21:50–22:00) via darker harmonic bed and wider spatial field.
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor cue paired with carrier release at first pairing, then withheld until depth thresholds.
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Final fade with harmonic simplification (F root only) and spatial contraction.
- Anti-padding rule: No looped events; all changes are deliberate micro-developments.

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: Ground drone (F2, 87.31 Hz) with slow beating.
- Harmonic color function: Harmonic body layer (F-centered open voicings).
- Motion function: Carrier layer (voice-adjacent, breath-length phrases).
- Foreground / event function: Event layer (rare motif fragments and partial blooms).
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Carrier layer (sarangi-like, downward settling phrases).
- Anchor / trigger function: Anchor layer (low-mid warmth bloom at carrier release points).
- Air / bloom function: Air layer (filtered high air, slow opening/closing).
- Spatial / depth function: Stereo width and depth (narrow to wide to narrow arc).
- Silence / restraint function: Withholding of clear motifs and fractionation brightness until core.

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One meaningful emergence per 15–45 seconds (e.g., motif fragment, anchor cue).
- Scene-change rate: Every 75–150 seconds (harmonic region shifts, spatial changes).
- Long-wave change: Harmonic bed pitch descent from 87.31 Hz to 123.47 Hz over core, then return.
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without meaningful change (e.g., no event, but modulation continues).
- Repetition policy: Recurrence of motif transformed (not identical) at depth thresholds.
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 21:45–21:50, re-deepening 21:50–22:00.
- Anchor recurrence spacing: First pairing (2:15), induction threshold (4:30), deepening stability (10:00), core reassurance (17:30), fractionation re-deepening (21:55), integration (22:45).

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: Stereo.
- Spatial arc: Narrow (0:00–3:00) → Medium (3:00–7:00) → Wide (7:00–13:00) → Medium-wide (13:00–21:45) → Narrow (21:45–24:00).
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered, harmonic body wide stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges and rear.
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; spatial movement over 20–120 seconds only for subtle drift.
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, dual-mono; width arc audible and motion not alerting.

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No perceptible change for >45 seconds; harmonic bed unchanged.
  - Revision target: Add micro-event (e.g., air layer opening) or harmonic shift at 75-second intervals.
- Failure mode: too generic
  - Evidence: Could be any ambient piece; no specific motif or harmonic signature.
  - Revision target: Add primary motif (C3-Ab2-G2) and transform it at each depth threshold.
- Failure mode: too busy
  - Evidence: More than one event per 15 seconds; carrier becomes lead.
  - Revision target: Reduce event density to one emergence per 60 seconds; lower carrier level.
- Failure mode: weak recurrence
  - Evidence: Motif appears once and vanishes; no transformed return.
  - Revision target: Return motif at each depth threshold (e.g., lowered in deepening).
- Failure mode: weak ending
  - Evidence: Simple fade-out without harmonic simplification.
  - Revision target: Add final cadence with F root only and spatial contraction to narrow.
- Failure mode: weak hypnotic architecture
  - Evidence: No clear stage progression; depth trajectory flat.
  - Revision target: Map harmonic bed pitch to depth (87.31 Hz = baseline, 123.47 Hz = deep).
- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like reset; re-deepening not deeper.
  - Revision target: Bind lightening to brief brightness increase (21:45–21:50) and re-deepening to darker harmonic bed (21:50–22:00).
- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor cues random or too obvious.
  - Revision target: Pair anchor with carrier release at first pairing (2:15), then withhold until depth thresholds.
