## Artistic Reading

- User image / premise: A continuous breath-led instrumental voice guiding self-hypnotic journey through structured stages
- Musical problem: Creating non-intrusive lead that guides without demanding attention while maintaining hypnotic depth trajectory
- Listener stance: Following the continuous breath-length phrases in a calm, absorbed state
- What this is not: Pop song structure, cinematic tension bed, EDM groove, or foreground melody
- Style-family fit: Neuro-ambient with voice-adjacent induction (primary style family) and spectral color, process_and_phasing

## Chosen Musical Premise

- Premise sentence: A continuous breath-led instrumental voice guides the listener through a self-hypnotic journey using weighted cadence and layered ambient textures to deepen absorption without interruption
- Primary dramatic motion: Establish → deepen → fractionate (briefly lighten) → stabilize → return
- Sound-world metaphor: A slow, warm river flowing through a forest of deepening shadows
- Musical consequence: Harmonic shift from F2 home color to F# minor contrast during deepening; texture evolves from sparse substrate to layered micro-developments; spatial arc moves from narrow to wide then back
- Duration rationale: 24 minutes allows earned depth progression (6-minute deepening), sustained core work (7 minutes), and gentle fractionation without rushing

## Hypnotic Function Architecture

- Target use: Music-only self-hypnosis (no spoken words)
- Stage map:
  - Orientation / Settling: 3:00, baseline_to_light (establish world and first carrier pairing)
  - Induction / Narrowing: 4:00, light_to_medium (pace and lead attention inward)
  - Deepening: 6:00, medium_to_deep (widen space and lower salience)
  - Core Absorptive Work: 7:00, deep (sustain absorption with micro-developments)
  - Fractionation Pocket: 2:00, deep_to_light_medium_to_deep (briefly lighten then return deeper)
  - Integration / Stabilization: 1:30, medium_to_light (return familiar material in calmer form)
  - Return / Reorientation: 0:30, light_to_baseline (dissolve and cadence)
- Depth trajectory: Baseline → light via stable F2 drone; light → medium via rhythmic carrier pacing; medium → deep via harmonic shift to F# minor and register lowering; deep maintained through core; deep → light_medium via brief bed brightening (3500Hz); light_medium → light via harmonic return to F2; light → baseline via carrier dissolution
- Pacing-and-leading plan: Carrier phrase length (6-14s) and breath pauses pace attention inward; induction uses regular 8-10s phrases to narrow focus
- Ratification plan: Downward settling contour (84.5% falling-settling) at phrase end ratifies state
- Suggestion-compatible windows: Quiet moments after carrier phrases (during breath pauses)
- Reset avoidance rule: No abrupt cuts, hard transients, or sudden brightness spikes; all transitions use 12-second crossfades

## Material Inventory

### Material Inventory - Primary Motif / Gesture
- Identity: 3-note descending interval cell (F-A-C) with third note held longer
- First form: Orientation phrase (F-A-C) with 2-second breath pause
- Later forms: Deepening transposes to E-G-B (F# minor); core uses fragment (A-C) with longer decay

### Material Inventory - Harmonic Signature
- Home color: F2 + 4th (Bb) + minor7 (Eb) — dark warm F major 7th
- Contrast color: F# minor (F#-A-C) — darker harmonic shift for deepening
- Return color: F2 + 4th (Bb) with softened minor7 to major7 (E natural)

### Material Inventory - Texture Family
- Continuous layer behavior: Slow-breathing ground drone (attack 10s, release 45s) with 0.2Hz beating
- Event layer behavior: Submerged motif fragments (every 60-90s) that bloom and recede
- Noise / air behavior: High-frequency air layer (filtered at 10kHz) that opens slowly during deepening

### Material Inventory - Spatial Material
- Width behavior: Narrow (0-3min) → medium (3-7min) → wide (7-13min) → medium-wide (13-20min) → narrow (20-24min)
- Motion behavior: Air layer drifts left to right during deepening; carrier remains near center
- Depth behavior: Bed wide/stable, air layer moves from rear to front during deepening

### Material Inventory - Modulation Identity
- Rate family: Slow breath (0.1-0.2 Hz) for ground drone; secondary shimmer (0.35-0.4 Hz) in air layer
- Change rule: Modulation rates slow during deepening/core, speed slightly in fractionation

### Material Inventory - Hypnotist-Voice Analog
- Reference cadence profile: reference_voice_cadence_profile_v1
- Reference pitch-contour profile: reference_voice_pitch_contours_v1 (84.5% falling-settling)
- Carrier instrument / layer: Soft cello with breathy low-mid resonance (GM 89, filtered for warmth)
- Cadence behavior: Breath-length phrases (6-14s) with 1.0-3.5s breath pauses; gentle downward settling
- Contour behavior: 84.5% falling-settling, occasional rising-openings (fractionation), arches (integration)
- Weighting behavior: Slight emphasis on second note; soft attack, long release
- Return behavior: Orientation contour returns in integration with softer register
- Salience limit: Carrier ≥6dB above harmonic bed, ≥10dB above substrate

### Material Inventory - Nonverbal Anchors And Subtle Triggers
- Anchor identity: Low-mid warmth bloom (0.5s) with slight widening at phrase end
- First pairing: Orientation (1:45), carrier settles downward → anchor bloom appears as comfort cue
- Withholding plan: Absent during induction (builds expectation for deepening return)
- Return / trigger plan: Deepening stability (10:30), fractionation re-deepening (21:00), integration reassurance (22:45)
- Salience policy: Audible enough to learn (3dB above bed), not salient enough for monitoring

## Development Grammar

- Establishment rule: Introduce carrier and ground drone with sparse phrases (orientation)
- Variation rule: Carrier contour varies by stage; harmonic color shifts
- Contrast rule: Home (F2) to contrast (F# minor) via harmonic shift, not abrupt change
- Recurrence rule: Primary motif returns transformed (transposed, longer decay)
- Foreground rule: Carrier always foreground (≥6dB above bed) in every section
- Withholding rule: Anchor withheld during induction to build meaning for return
- Fractionation rule: Brief bed brightening (3500Hz) for 15s, then return to deep (2600Hz)
- Trigger conditioning rule: Anchor paired with downward carrier phrase → becomes associated with comfort
- Dissolution / cadence rule: Return section dissolves carrier into bed over 30s
- Anti-padding rule: No event repeated without transformation; minimum 60s spacing

## Orchestration Map

- Root / ground function: Ground drone (F2, 0.2Hz beating)
- Harmonic color function: Harmonic body layer (F-centered open voicings, slow filter motion)
- Motion function: Carrier layer (breath-length phrases with weighted cadence)
- Foreground / event function: Event layer (rare motif fragments, anchor blooms)
- Voice-adjacent induction function: Carrier layer (soft cello with breathy resonance, 6-14s phrases)
- Anchor / trigger function: Anchor layer (low-mid warmth bloom, 0.5s)
- Air / bloom function: Air layer (filtered high air at edges, slow opening/closing)
- Spatial / depth function: Layer placement (ground centered, harmonic body wide, carrier near center, air at edges)
- Silence / restraint function: Anchor withheld during induction

## Temporal Behavior

- Micro-event rate: One event (motif fragment or anchor) every 60-90 seconds
- Scene-change rate: Every stage transition (3,4,6,7,2,1.5 minutes)
- Long-wave change: Harmonic shift from home to contrast (deepening) and back (integration)
- Maximum stasis tolerance: 45 seconds without meaningful change
- Repetition policy: Motif returns transformed, not repeated identically
- Fractionation timing: Lightening 20:00-21:30, return to deep 21:30-22:00
- Anchor recurrence spacing: Orientation (1:45), deepening stability (10:30), fractionation re-deepening (21:00), integration reassurance (22:45)

## Spatial Composition

- Stereo requirement: stereo
- Spatial arc: narrow → medium → wide → medium-wide → narrow
- Layer placement principles: Ground centered, harmonic body wide stable, carrier near center low motion, air at edges/rear
- Motion limits: No fast autopan; spatial movement over 20-120 seconds (e.g., air drift takes 6 minutes)
- Verification obligation: Verify not mono, width arc audible and motion not alerting

## Revision Criteria

- Failure mode: too static
  - Evidence: No harmonic shift or texture change for >45 seconds
  - Revision target: Add micro-event (motif fragment) or harmonic shift

- Failure mode: weak fractionation
  - Evidence: Lightening feels like reset (sudden brightness)
  - Revision target: Ensure bed filter returns to deep (2600Hz) without abrupt change

- Failure mode: weak anchors / triggers
  - Evidence: Anchor cues too obvious or random (e.g., bright, long)
  - Revision target: Make bloom softer (0.5s, 3dB above bed) and pair with downward carrier phrase

- Failure mode: weak voice-adjacent induction
  - Evidence: Carrier phrases mechanical or too short (<6s)
  - Revision target: Use empirical profile (6-14s, falling-settling) and soften attacks
