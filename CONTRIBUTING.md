# Contributing

This repository is **publish / reference only**. It does not accept
contributions.

## No pull requests, patches, or issues

We do not accept pull requests, patches, or issue reports against this
repository, and there is no contributor sign-off (DCO) process. Please do not
open one — it will not be reviewed or merged.

## Fork and grow your own

This is a sealed, public-safe homunculus seed — a snapshot, not a shared
development tree. A homunculus is meant to be *grown*, not
collaboratively edited: **fork this seed and grow your own** homunculus from it.
Your instance evolves on your own machine, as its own entity.

## Updates

Improvements to the seed are published here as **new sealed commits**, never as
merges of external contributions.
